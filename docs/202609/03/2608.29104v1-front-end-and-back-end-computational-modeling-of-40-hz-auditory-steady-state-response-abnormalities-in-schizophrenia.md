## 摘要
40 Hz听觉稳态反应在精神分裂症中减弱，但尚不清楚这反映的是听觉输入改变还是皮层兴奋/抑制动态改变。我们假设类似的组间差异可能通过不同的模型机制产生。来自21名健康对照和21名精神分裂症患者的脑电gamma百分比和试次间相位一致性约束了一个与Wilson-Cowan兴奋/抑制模型耦合的听觉前端。我们比较了仅前端限制、仅后端限制和全联合参数搜索，并进行了扰动和不动点分析。健康对照组的均值在两项指标上均高于精神分裂症组（但单独比较均不显著）。三种模型均重现了健康对照大于精神分裂症的模式，但对组间差异的定位不同：前端模型将其定位于输入转换，后端模型将其定位于皮层动态，全联合模型则两者兼有。全联合解对扰动最为稳健。不动点分析显示输出相似但局部动态不同。所有模型均重现了健康对照大于精神分裂症的模式，提示精神分裂症的病理生理可能涉及感觉编码改变、皮层兴奋/抑制改变或两者兼有。该框架支持未来进行患者层面的机制比较，并在验证后可能有助于个体化分层。

## Abstract
40-Hz ASSR is reduced in schizophrenia, but it is unclear if this reflects altered auditory input or cortical E/I dynamics. We hypothesized that similar group differences could arise via distinct model mechanisms. EEG gamma% and ITPC from 21 HC and 21 SCZ constrained an auditory front-end coupled to a Wilson-Cowan E/I model. We compared front-end-restricted, back-end-restricted, and full-joint parameter searches, plus perturbation and fixed-point analyses. HC means exceeded SCZ for both metrics (not individually significant). All three models reproduced HC>SCZ but located group differences differently: front-end via input transformation, back-end via cortical dynamics, full-joint via both. The full-joint solution was most robust to perturbation. Fixed-point analysis revealed similar outputs with distinct local dynamics. All models reproduced the HC>SCZ pattern, suggesting schizophrenia pathophysiology may involve altered sensory encoding, altered cortical E/I, or both. This framework enables future patient-level mechanistic comparison and, after validation, may support individualized stratification.
