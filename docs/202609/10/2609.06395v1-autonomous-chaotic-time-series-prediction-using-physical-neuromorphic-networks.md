## 摘要
利用神经形态网络进行物理储备池计算（PRC）为受脑启发的信息处理提供了一种有前景的方法，它将物理神经网络涌现的非线性动力学用作计算资源。本研究使用模拟的神经形态纳米线网络作为物理储备池，展示了 Mackey–Glass (MG) 混沌时间序列的全自主闭环预测。评估了两种策略：虚拟节点（VN）方法，通过对储备池状态进行时间多路复用来扩展特征空间；以及非 VN 方法，直接使用所有物理节点读出而不进行时间多路复用。报告了 MG 时间延迟参数两个取值 τ=18 和 τ=21 的结果，后者代表了一种此前未在此类物理储备池上评估过的更复杂混沌状态。在 T = 100 个时间步的短期预测范围内，VN 方法在 τ=18 和 τ=21 时分别实现了 90.4% 和 89.7% 的自主预测准确率，而非 VN 方法分别实现了 81.5% 和 76.2%。在 T = 500 个时间步的长期分析表明，两种方法都再现了真实 MG 信号的定性吸引子结构和主要频谱内容，且轨迹始终保持有界。这些结果表明，神经形态纳米线网络的内禀动力学足以在没有虚拟节点增强的情况下支持有意义的自主混沌时间序列预测，并且随着物理网络规模扩展到硬件中可实现的数百万个节点，性能可能进一步提高。由于本研究使用模拟网络，将其外推到物理制备的大规模阵列仍有待实验验证。

## Abstract
Physical reservoir computing (PRC) with neuromorphic networks offers a promising approach to brain-inspired information processing, exploiting emergent nonlinear dynamics of physical neural networks as a computational resource. This study demonstrates fully autonomous closed-loop prediction of the Mackey--Glass (MG) chaotic time series using a simulated neuromorphic nanowire network as the physical reservoir. Two strategies are evaluated: the virtual node (VN) method, which expands the feature space by temporal multiplexing of reservoir states, and a non-VN approach that uses all physical node readouts directly without temporal multiplexing. Results are reported for two values of the MG time delay parameter, $τ= 18$ and $τ= 21$, the latter representing a more complex chaotic regime not previously evaluated for this class of physical reservoir. Over a short prediction horizon of $T = 100$ timesteps, the VN approach achieves autonomous prediction accuracies of $90.4$% and $89.7$% at $τ= 18$ and $τ= 21$, respectively, while the non-VN approach achieves $81.5$% and $76.2$%. Long-horizon analysis over $T = 500$ timesteps shows that both approaches reproduce the qualitative attractor structure and dominant spectral content of the true MG signal, with trajectories remaining bounded throughout. These results suggest that the intrinsic dynamics of neuromorphic nanowire networks are sufficient to support meaningful autonomous chaotic time series prediction without virtual node augmentation, and that performance may improve further as physical network sizes scale to the millions of nodes achievable in hardware. As this study uses simulated networks, extrapolation to physically fabricated large-scale arrays remains to be validated experimentally.
