## 摘要
交感节前神经元（SPN）是中枢神经系统调控自主功能的最终通路。投射至椎旁交感神经链神经节的SPN轴突广泛分支并跨多个节段发散，通过数量庞大的节后神经元群体放大中枢交感指令。一般认为锋电位沿这些投射的传播是可靠的。然而，大多数SPN轴突是极细的无髓纤维，这种结构特征预计会降低锋电位传播的安全系数。

利用离体小鼠胸段交感链标本，我们结合解剖示踪与多位点复合动作电位记录，评估了SPN轴突的传导。神经生物素标记揭示了经节间神经广泛的头-尾侧发散，而轴突测量确认大多数SPN轴突是小直径无髓纤维。在所有标本中，SPN的超强刺激招募揭示了复合反应在试验间存在显著变异性，提示频繁的传导失败。失败在慢传导轴突中最为突出，且同时发生于分支的节间通路和内脏神经内的无分支轴突。在重复激活期间，在1、5和10 Hz下观察到频率依赖性抑制，但只有慢传导的分支轴突表现出明显的抑制。

总体而言，这些发现表明SPN轴突中的锋电位传播可能以概率性而非确定性的方式运作，其可靠性高度依赖于轴突亚型和近期活动历史。我们得出结论：轴突传导变异性构成了一种内在且动态调节的机制，塑造交感输出。通过改变节后群体的招募，SPN轴突中不可靠的锋电位传播引入了一种先前未被识别的突触前增益控制机制，该机制独立于中枢锋电位产生，调节交感输出。

意义 交感节前神经元提供了中枢神经系统控制终末器官的最终通路。这些神经元经交感链投射，其轴突在此广泛分支，以招募更多的椎旁节后神经元。一般认为锋电位沿这些投射的传播是可靠的。本研究表明这一假设是不正确的。利用小鼠交感链标本的解剖示踪和电生理记录，我们证明交感节前轴突中的锋电位传导经常可变且易于失败，尤其是在最慢传导的无髓纤维中。在重复激活期间，分支轴突通路中的传导变异性被优先增强。这些发现揭示，轴突传导可靠性是调节交感输出幅度和变异性的一个重要突触前机制。

## Abstract
Sympathetic preganglionic neurons (SPNs) provide the final pathway through which the central nervous system regulates autonomic function. SPN axons projecting to paravertebral sympathetic chain ganglia branch extensively and diverge across multiple segments, enabling amplification of central sympathetic commands through extensive postganglionic neuronal populations. Spike propagation along these projections has generally been assumed to occur reliably. However, most SPN axons are extremely small unmyelinated fibers, a structural feature predicted to reduce the safety factor for spike propagation.

Using an isolated mouse thoracic sympathetic chain preparation, we combined anatomical tracing with multi-site compound action potential recordings to assess conduction across SPN axons. Neurobiotin labeling revealed widespread rostrocaudal divergence through interganglionic nerves, while axon measurements confirmed that most SPN axons are small unmyelinated fibers. Across preparations, supramaximal recruitment of SPNs revealed substantial intertrial variability in compound responses, indicating frequent conduction failures. Failures were most prominent in slow-conducting axons and occurred in both branching interganglionic pathways and the unbranching axons within the splanchnic nerve. During repetitive activation, frequency dependent depression was observed at 1, 5 and 10Hz, but only slow-conducting branching axons exhibited pronounced depression.

Overall, these findings indicate that spike propagation in SPN axons may operate probabilistically rather than deterministically, with reliability strongly dependent on axonal subtype and recent activity history. We conclude that axonal conduction variability constitutes an intrinsic and dynamically regulated mechanism that shapes sympathetic output. By varying the recruitment of postganglionic populations, unreliable spike propagation in SPN axons introduces a previously unrecognized presynaptic gain-control mechanism, operating independently of central spike generation to modulate sympathetic output.

SIGNIFICANCESympathetic preganglionic neurons provide the final pathway through which the central nervous system controls end-organs. These neurons project through the sympathetic chain where their axons branch extensively to recruit more numerous paravertebral postganglionic neurons. Spike propagation along these projections has generally been assumed to occur reliably. Here we show that this assumption is incorrect. Using anatomical tracing and electrophysiological recordings in mouse sympathetic chain preparations, we demonstrate that spike conduction in sympathetic preganglionic axons is frequently variable and prone to failure, particularly in the slowest-conducting unmyelinated fibers. Conduction variability was preferentially enhanced in branching axonal pathways during repetitive activation. These findings reveal that axonal conduction reliability represents an important presynaptic mechanism regulating the magnitude and variability of sympathetic output.
