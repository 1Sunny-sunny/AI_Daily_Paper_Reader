## 摘要
最佳利用感觉信息需要大脑在表征感觉变量的同时表征不确定性。然而，介导可靠性编码的神经反应特性仍未被明确。通过对初级视觉皮层（V1）和高级视觉区（HVAs）中的140,000个神经元进行大规模双光子钙成像，并在呈现随机点运动图刺激期间，我们发现了一种共同的编码策略，即感觉可靠性通过反应增益、选择性和变异性的协调变化来表征。这些反应特性在单神经元和群体水平上均有表达，并且在不同脑区之间是一致的，其幅度的定量差异反映了视网膜拓扑驱动的输入和功能特化。总之，我们的结果确立了可靠性编码是一种通过共享群体编码实现的通用皮层计算，为感知的概率理论和视觉处理的层级模型提供了经验约束。

## Abstract
Optimal use of sensory information requires the brain to represent uncertainty alongside sensory variables. Yet, the neural response properties that mediate reliability encoding remain undefined. Using large-scale two-photon calcium imaging of 140,000 neurons in primary visual cortex (V1) and higher visual areas (HVAs) during presentation of random dot kine-matograms, we identified a common coding strategy in which sensory reliability is represented via coordinated changes in response gain, selectivity, and variability. These response properties are expressed at both single neuron and population levels and are consistent across areas, with quantitative differences in magnitude that track retinotopic drive and functional specialization. Together, our results establish reliability encoding as a general cortical computation implemented through a shared population code, providing empirical constraints on probabilistic theories of perception and hierarchical models of visual processing.
