## 摘要
海马是内侧颞叶中的关键结构，在陈述性记忆的形成和巩固中起着核心作用。尽管技术进步，但由于其牛角面包状的复杂形态限制了冠状和矢状入路，对海马节律、神经元群体活动及潜在连接性的全面理解仍然难以实现。为了解决这一局限，我们提出了两种不同的高密度电极探针切向植入方法，分别沿海马背侧或腹侧部分对齐。每种方法都能在海马结构内放置多达384个记录位点。两种植入均在头部固定条件下进行，这进一步允许对动物的行为状态进行精细监测，包括自发奔跑和面部运动。所提出的两种背侧和腹侧切向植入几乎连续地捕获了海马3.84毫米组织范围内的生理活动。当穿过锥体细胞层时，此类植入可以在120个通道上利用涟漪振荡，对应约1.2毫米的组织，每20微米覆盖2个通道。此外，这种植入方法使我们能够从单个探针同时记录多达400个海马神经元，从而能够单次记录数百个功能连接的神经元对。最后，这些植入是在头部固定于跑步带上的小鼠中进行的，可与高频采样相机结合，使我们能够精确监测面部运动及其海马关联。正交/垂直入路是记录包括海马在内的大多数脑区电生理活动的一种更标准的方式。然而，垂直植入会错过海马脑区的前后轴，因此可能遗漏传播事件。此外，就记录的通道数量、单个单元和空间密度而言，垂直植入的覆盖范围较小，即使是使用多柄高密度探针也是如此。因此，切向植入显著增加了空间覆盖范围和分辨率，从而在海马前后轴上单次植入记录到更多的细胞。此外，与自由活动方法相比，头部固定条件能够精确监测面部运动。在海马中用Neuropixels探针进行的背侧和腹侧头部固定切向植入提高了记录的分辨率和空间范围，使得能够更精确、更广泛地监测网络活动及其潜在连接性。

## Abstract
The hippocampus is a key structure within the medial temporal lobe which plays a central role in the formation and consolidation of declarative memories. Despite technological progress, a full understanding of hippocampal rhythms, neuronal population activity, and the underlying connectivity remain elusive, partly due to its complex morphology in a croissant-like shape which limits coronal and sagittal approaches. To address this limitation, we propose two different tangential insertions of high-density electrode probes aligned to either the dorsal or ventral sections of the hippocampus. Each approach enables the placement of up to 384 recording sites within the hippocampal formation. Both insertions are performed in head-fixed condition, which further allows for detailed monitoring of the animals behavioral states, including spontaneous running and facial movements. The two proposed tangential dorsal and ventral insertions capture almost continuously the physiology of the hippocampus across 3.84 mm of tissue. When placed across the pyramidal layer, such insertions can harness ripple oscillations over 120 channels, corresponding to approximately ~1.2 mm of tissue, with a coverage of 2 channels every 20 micrometer. Furthermore, such insertions enabled us to record up to 400 hippocampal neurons simultaneously from a single probe, endowing us with the capability to capture within a single recording several hundreds of functionally connected neuronal pairs. Lastly, these insertions were performed in head-fixed mice on a running band, which can be combined with high frequency sampling camera, allowing us to precisely monitor facial movements, and their hippocampal correlates. An orthogonal/vertical approach is a more standard way to record electrophysiological activity from most brain regions, including the hippocampus. Vertical insertions thereby miss the antero-posterior axis of the brain in the hippocampus and therefore putative propagating events. Furthermore, vertical insertions have a lower reach in terms of number of channels, single units, and spatial density being recorded, even with multi-shank high-density probes. Tangential insertions therefore substantially increase spatial reach and resolution, which leads to a higher number of cells recorded in a single insertion along the antero-posterior axis of the hippocampus. Furthermore, compared to freely moving approaches, head-fixed conditions enable precise monitoring of facial movements. Dorsal and ventral head-fixed tangential insertions of Neuropixels probes in the hippocampus increase the resolution and spatial extent of recordings, allowing more precise and extensive monitoring of network activity and its underlying connectivity.
