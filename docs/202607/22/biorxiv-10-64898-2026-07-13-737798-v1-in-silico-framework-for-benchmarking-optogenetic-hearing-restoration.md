## 摘要
背景：人工耳蜗（CI）通过电刺激听神经，部分恢复了重度听力受损或聋人的听力。电CI的一个瓶颈是每个电极产生的电流扩散较广，限制了频谱信息的传递，而未来空间受限的光遗传学刺激有望克服这一局限。目的：在此我们建立了一个名为FraSCO的计算机模拟框架，用于模拟光遗传学CI（oCI）在人耳蜗中的声音编码，以测试光遗传学听力恢复的潜力。方法：该生物物理建模框架结合了光学光线追踪模型（模拟植入基于波导的oCI的人耳蜗）与光遗传学改造的螺旋神经节神经元（SGNs）的单室模型。输入为光遗传学声音编码策略，通过比较由光遗传学和电刺激诱发的神经图与所施加声音的频谱图，评估神经表征的质量。该模型旨在实现具有64个刺激通道的oCI的技术可行特性。结果：生物物理建模框架通过最小种类的SGN胞体中表达的离子通道类型，成功捕捉了光遗传学SGN刺激的基本生理特征。利用沿频率拓扑轴分布的1000个SGN样本进行声音编码，我们发现，改进的频谱选择性足以补偿当前光遗传学刺激实现中较低的时间保真度。意义：所建立的计算框架使得未来oCI和最先进的eCI在耳蜗中声音编码的计算机模拟研究与基准测试成为可能。结果表明，光遗传学声音编码有潜力改善CI用户在嘈杂环境中的言语理解。

## Abstract
Background. Cochlear implants (CI) partially restore hearing in profoundly hearing im-paired or deaf people by electrically stimulating the auditory nerve. A bottleneck of electri-cal CIs is the broad spread of electrical current from each electrode that limits the transfer of spectral information, which might be overcome by future spatially confined optogenetic stimulation. Objective. Here we established an in silico framework, FraSCO, to model sound encoding in the human cochlea by an optogenetic CI (oCI) for testing the potential of optogenetic hearing restoration. Methods. The biophysical modeling framework combined an optical ray tracing model implementing a human cochlea implanted with a waveguide-based oCI with a single compartment model of optogenetically modified spiral ganglion neurons (SGNs). The input was an optogenetic sound coding strategy and the quality of the neural representation was evaluated based on comparison of neurograms evoked by opto-genetic and electrical stimulation to the spectrogram of the sound applied. The model aimed for technologically feasible properties of the oCIs with 64 stimulation channels. Re-sults. The biophysical modeling framework successfully captured essential physiological features of optogenetic SGN stimulation with a minimal set of ion channel types expressed in the SGN soma. Working with a sample of 1000 SGNs distributed along the tonotopic ax-is to represent sound encoding, we found that improved spectral selectivity more than com-pensates for lower temporal fidelity of current implementations of optogenetic stimulation. Significance. The established computational framework enables in silico investigation and benchmarking of sound encoding in the cochlea by future oCI and state-of-the-art eCI. The results indicate that optogenetic sound encoding has potential to improve speech under-standing in noisy environments for CI users.
