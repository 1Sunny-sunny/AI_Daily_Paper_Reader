## 摘要
许多生物学功能是由感知与驱动之间的反馈驱动的。一个典型的例子是细胞导航。在导航过程中，感觉系统将环境输入信号映射为感觉输出，然后驱动一个动作响应，改变未来的感觉输入。这种双向信息传递的准确性如何控制导航，目前尚不清楚。在这里，我们通过解析求解两个通用模型来研究信息如何控制导航，这两个模型描述了生物导航器的两大类：空间感知细胞和时间感知细胞。我们发现，在浅梯度的线性响应区域中，导航性能完全由双向信息传递的强度和时标决定，而不明确依赖于导航器的基本参数。我们将这些关系称为行为状态方程（BESTs）：以系统无关的方式将信息映射为功能的等式。BESTs预测了具有不同感知和驱动参数的导航器性能的实验可测试的数据坍缩现象。我们通过对大肠杆菌的趋化性进行随机模拟，利用TE-PWS算法精确计算传递熵，来检验我们理论的有效性。观察到的性能在没有任何拟合或缩放参数的情况下服从BEST。因此，我们的理论将感知与驱动之间的双向信息传递确定为导航的组织原则。

## Abstract
A wide variety of biological functions are driven by feedback between sensing and actuation. A paradigmatic example is cellular navigation. During navigation, the sensory system maps the environmental input signal onto a sensory output, which then drives an actuation response, changing the future sensory input. How the accuracy of this bidirectional information transmission controls navigation is not currently understood. Here, we study how information controls navigation by analytically solving two generic models that describe two major classes of biological navigators: spatial- and temporal-sensing cells. We find that, in the linear-response regime of shallow gradients, navigation performance is fully determined by the strengths and timescales of bidirectional information transmission, without any explicit dependence on the elementary parameters of the navigator. We call these relations Behavioral Equations of State (BESTs): equalities that map information to function in a system-independent way. BESTs predict an experimentally testable data collapse for the performance of navigators with different sensing and actuation parameters. We test the validity of our theory by performing stochastic simulations of chemotaxis of the bacterium Escherichia coli, computing transfer entropies exactly with the TE-PWS algorithm. The observed performance obeys the BEST without fitting or scaling parameters. Thus, our theory identifies bidirectional information transmission between sensing and actuation as an organizing principle for navigation.
