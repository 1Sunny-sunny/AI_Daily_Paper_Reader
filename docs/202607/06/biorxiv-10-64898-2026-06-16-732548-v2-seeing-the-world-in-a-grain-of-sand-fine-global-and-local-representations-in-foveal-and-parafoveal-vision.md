## 摘要
中心凹视觉使灵长类动物能够以极高的精度感知小物体，同时解析全局形状和精细局部细节。然而，当前从粗到细和从局部到整体的框架，主要源自中心凹旁记录和大物体表征，假设V1编码局部细节而IT表征全局形状，使得中心凹物体表征的神经基础悬而未决。在此，我们展示精细全局结构和精细局部细节早在V1就联合编码，并在视网膜离心度为中心凹（1°-2°）和中心凹旁（2°-6°）的V2、V4和IT中得到保留。相比之下，粗糙全局特征在中心凹旁视觉和更高级皮层区域占主导地位。反应潜伏期揭示了快速的加工序列，在中心凹旁V1、V2和V4中，粗糙全局、精细局部和精细全局加工之间间隔2-6毫秒。这些发现建立了一个神经框架，其中连贯的整体和组成部分在中心凹视觉中联合表征，支持阅读和精细物体识别过程中的精细尺度感知。

## Abstract
Foveal vision enables primates to perceive small objects with remarkable precision by resolving both global form and fine local detail. Yet current coarse-to-fine and local-to-global frameworks, derived largely from parafoveal recordings and large-object representations, posit that V1 encodes local detail whereas IT represents global form, leaving the neuronal basis of foveal object representation unresolved. Here we show that fine-global structure and fine-local detail are jointly encoded as early as V1 and preserved across V2, V4 and IT at both foveal (1{degrees}-2{degrees}) and parafoveal (2{degrees}-6{degrees}) retinal eccentricities. By contrast, coarse-global features dominated parafoveal vision and higher cortical areas. Response latencies revealed a rapid processing sequence, with 2-6 ms separating coarse-global, fine-local, and fine-global processing within parafoveal V1, V2 and V4. These findings establish a neuronal framework in which coherent wholes and constituent parts are jointly represented in foveal vision, supporting fine-scale perception during reading and fine object recognition.
