## 摘要
生物神经元通过分流在分支树突上结合兴奋性和抑制性(E/I)活动，其中抑制以除法方式衰减兴奋。这是否比相同非负输入的加性E/I整合提高了群体读出的性能尚不清楚。我们引入了DendriNet，一个可训练的框架，它变化整合规则、形态学、突触分配、除数局部性和树突非线性。对于具有乘性增益的群体编码，任何可实现的分流读出的局部线性化会在正加性E/I锥体内产生一个决策方向；匹配加性最优解需要一个正的自洽分流实现。每个标量分流阈值也有一个精确的仿射加性实现。在此局部极限之外，性能遵循增益-负载-对齐原理：当可靠的除数抑制信号对齐的增益超过它衰减信号或增加分母变异性时，分支局部分流有帮助。无源加性树会退化为线性读出，而分流树则组合局部除数。在一个设计的分层结构中，深度分流优于切线和拟合线性对照，但灵活的非线性预测器在标签足够多时会超过它。支持打乱反转了线性比较，传感器损坏反转了拟合线性比较，而资源匹配的激活训练没有显示出一致的深度优势。在冻结特征归一化中出现了同样的支持和可靠性交互。在三个小鼠V1会话中，分流相对于加性解码器的差距在窄读出时最大，在最宽读出时在强私有噪声下反转，并且随跑步状态变化。形态学可以确定可靠的干扰估计在哪里与任务相关信号相遇，但深度和分流本质上都不是绝对优势。

## Abstract
Biological neurons combine excitatory and inhibitory (E/I) activity on branched dendrites through shunting, in which inhibition divisively attenuates excitation. Whether this improves population readout over additive E/I integration of the same nonnegative inputs remains unclear. We introduce DendriNet, a trainable framework that varies integration rule, morphology, synaptic allocation, divisor locality, and dendritic nonlinearities. For population codes with multiplicative gain, a local linearization of any realizable shunting readout yields a decision direction within the positive additive E/I cone; matching the additive optimum requires a positive self-consistent shunting realization. Every scalar shunting threshold also has an exact affine additive realization. Beyond this local limit, performance follows a gain-load-alignment principle: branch-local shunting helps when a reliable divisor suppresses signal-aligned gain more than it attenuates signal or adds denominator variability. Passive additive trees flatten to linear readouts, whereas shunting trees compose local divisors. In a designed hierarchy, deep shunting outperforms tangent and fitted-linear controls, but flexible nonlinear predictors overtake it with enough labels. Support shuffling reverses the linear comparisons, sensor corruption reverses the fitted-linear comparison, and resource-matched activated training shows no consistent depth benefit. The same support and reliability interaction appears in frozen-feature normalization. Across three mouse V1 sessions, the shunting-over-additive decoder gap is largest for narrow readouts, reverses under strong private noise at the widest readout, and varies across running states. Morphology can determine where reliable nuisance estimates meet task-relevant signals, but neither depth nor shunting is intrinsically advantageous.
