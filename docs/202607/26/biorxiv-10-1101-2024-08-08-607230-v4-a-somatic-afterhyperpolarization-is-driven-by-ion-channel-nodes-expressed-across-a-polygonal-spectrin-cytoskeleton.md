## 摘要
血影蛋白-肌动蛋白细胞骨架定义了轴突的结构和支持锋电位传播的离子通道表达，但尚不清楚钙通道和钾通道如何在胞体处组织以控制锋电位输出模式。海马锥体细胞的慢后超极化(sAHP)由Cav1.3钙通道、RyR2和IK钾通道组成的CaRyK蛋白复合物产生，反映了内质网-质膜连接。超分辨率成像和降维分析揭示，CaRyK蛋白簇高度有序地排列成行，具有约155纳米的周期性，并延伸至分支点，在胞体上形成非刚性晶格状结构。所有CaRyK蛋白均被证明与多边形血影蛋白βII细胞骨架对齐并共定位。数据表明，内质网-质膜连接处的CaRyK蛋白参与了sAHP以控制锋电位输出模式，它们作为功能性离子通道节点，以高精度分布在胞体血影蛋白细胞骨架网络上。

## Abstract
A spectrin-actin cytoskeleton defines the structure of axons and expression of ion channels that support spike propagation, but it is not known how calcium and potassium channels are organized at the soma to control spike output patterns. The hippocampal pyramidal cell slow afterhyperpolarization (sAHP) is generated by a CaRyK protein complex of Cav1.3 calcium, RyR2, and IK potassium channels reflecting an ER-PM junction. Super resolution imaging and dimension reduction identified a highly organized distribution of CaRyK protein clusters aligned as rows with ~155 nm periodicity that extended to branchpoints to form a non-rigid lattice-like structure across the soma. All CaRyK proteins proved to align and colocalize with the polygonal spectrin {beta}II cytoskeleton. The data indicate that CaRyK proteins at ER-PM junctions that contribute to the sAHP to control the pattern of spike output are distributed with high precision as functional ion channel nodes across the somatic spectrin cytoskeletal network.
