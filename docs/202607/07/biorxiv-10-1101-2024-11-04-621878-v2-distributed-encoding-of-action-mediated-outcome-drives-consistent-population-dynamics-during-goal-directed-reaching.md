## 摘要
预测动作结果是目标导向行为的核心，但在持续运动过程中，这些期望如何在全脑范围内编码仍不清楚。为了解决这一问题，我们在头部固定的小鼠执行伸爪取水任务时，使用多个Neuropixels探针同时记录了皮层和皮层下区域的放电活动。我们发现，分布式神经群体动力学除了编码前肢运动学外，还受到奖励可用性的强烈调节。主成分分析揭示了跨脑区和记录时段的保守群体动力学，这些动力学取决于伸爪幅度和奖励可用性。广义线性模型揭示了在区域特异性群体动力学中，除了运动学编码外，还存在与结果相关的编码，其中最强的结果信号出现在额叶皮层-丘脑区域。无监督聚类分析进一步识别出结果编码亚群，这些亚群在额叶皮层中富集，并对共享的全局潜在动力学做出了不成比例的巨大贡献。总之，这些发现表明，由动作介导的结果预期被编码在与运动相关的群体动力学中，而这些动力学是由全脑功能性神经元集群塑造的。

## Abstract
Anticipating the outcomes of actions is central to goal-directed behaviour, but how such expectations are encoded across the brain during ongoing movement remains unclear. To address this, we recorded spiking activity from cortical and subcortical regions using multiple Neuropixels probes simultaneously in head-fixed mice performing a water-reaching task. We found that distributed neural population dynamics were strongly modulated by the availability of reward beyond their encoding of forelimb kinematics. Principal component analysis revealed conserved population dynamics across brain regions and sessions that depended on reach amplitude and reward availability. Generalized linear models revealed outcome-related encoding within region-specific population dynamics, in addition to kinematic encoding, with the strongest outcome signals expressed in frontal cortico-thalamic regions. Unsupervised cluster analysis further identified outcome-encoding subpopulations that were enriched in frontal cortices and disproportionally contributed to the shared global latent dynamics. Together, these findings demonstrate that action-mediated outcome expectations are encoded in movement-related population dynamics that are shaped by functional clusters of neurons across the brain.
