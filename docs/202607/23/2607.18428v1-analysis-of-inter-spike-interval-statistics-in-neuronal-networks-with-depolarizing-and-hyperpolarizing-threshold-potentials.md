## 摘要
神经元交流部分通过放电率的变化来介导。连续神经元放电之间的时间间隔被称为峰间间隔（ISI），量化其统计特性对于理解神经交流至关重要。本文研究一个接受独立兴奋性和抑制性突触前动作电位（EI回路）的突触后神经元的ISI统计特性。该回路被建模为经典整合发放神经元，并针对固定和自适应阈值电位考察了ISI统计特性。首先研究了一个去极化自适应阈值模型，其中阈值电位随突触后膜电位升高而增加。分析表明，在相同平均ISI下，与固定阈值模型相比，自适应阈值模型的ISI噪声（以变异系数量化）更大。此外，模拟揭示，ISI噪声可以是亚指数或超指数的（分别定义为ISI噪声小于或大于一），取决于兴奋性和抑制性输入的频率。接下来研究了一个超极化自适应阈值电位，其中阈值随膜电位超极化而降低。有趣的是，该模型显示突触后神经元在仅受抑制性输入驱动时也能产生动作电位（AP）。进一步，我们刻画了兴奋性和抑制性输入下模型参数的平均值和噪声特征。总之，这项工作对动作电位产生的自适应阈值模型进行了系统的随机分析，以理解它们在神经元间信息处理中的作用。

## Abstract
Neuronal communication is mediated in part by changes in neuronal firing rates. The time interval between successive neuronal firings is referred to as the inter-spike interval (ISI), and quantifying its statistics is important for understanding neuronal communication. This paper studies the ISI statistics of a postsynaptic neuron receiving independent excitatory and inhibitory presynaptic action potentials (EI circuit). This circuit is modeled as a classical integrate-and-fire neuron, and the ISI statistics are investigated for both fixed and adaptive threshold potentials. First, a depolarizing adaptive threshold model is studied, where the threshold potential increases with the postsynaptic membrane potential. Our analysis shows that the ISI noise, quantified as the coefficient of variation, is larger in the adaptive threshold model compared to the fixed threshold model for the same mean ISI. Additionally, simulations reveal that the ISI noise can be either hypo- or hyper-exponential (defined as ISI noise smaller or larger than one, respectively) depending on the frequencies of excitatory and inhibitory inputs. Next, a hyperpolarizing adaptive threshold potential is studied, where the threshold decreases as the membrane potential hyperpolarizes. Interestingly, this model shows that the postsynaptic neuron can generate action potentials (APs) when driven solely by inhibitory inputs. Furthermore, mean and noise signatures are characterized across model parameters for both excitatory and inhibitory inputs. In summary, this work provides a systematic stochastic analysis of adaptive threshold models for AP generation to understand their role in interneuronal information processing.

### 主要结论与发现
*   **固定阈值模型**：抑制输入总是降低平均放电频率；当 $f_e$ 和 $f_i$ 均较大且兴奋稍占优时，ISI噪声最大。
*   **去极化自适应阈值模型**：
    *   与固定阈值模型相比，在相同平均ISI下，自适应阈值导致**更大的ISI噪声**（增加了变异性），且噪声峰值更高。
    *   ISI噪声在 $f_e \approx f_i$ 且两者都较大时**最大化**，导致 $CV^2_T > 1$（超指数分布），这与实验中观察到的兴奋-抑制平衡导致高度不规则放电的现象一致。
    *   平均放电频率对 $\tau_{th}$ 的敏感性在 $\tau_{th} < \tau_v$ 时才会体现，当 $\tau_{th} > \tau_v$ 时不敏感。
*   **超极化自适应阈值模型**：
    *   **仅抑制性输入即可诱发突触后AP**，模拟了后抑制反弹现象。
    *   存在**最优中间抑制频率**使得平均放电频率最大化，该最大值随 $\tau_v$ 增加而减小。
    *   平均放电频率在 $\tau_v$ 和 $\tau_{th}$ 均为中等大小时达到全局最大。
    *   ISI噪声在低 $\tau_v$ 和低 $\tau_{th}$ 时最大。
