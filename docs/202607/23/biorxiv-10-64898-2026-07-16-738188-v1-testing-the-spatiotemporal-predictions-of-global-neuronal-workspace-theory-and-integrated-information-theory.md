## 摘要
有意识知觉的神经机制仍存在争议，全局神经元工作空间理论（GNWT）和整合信息理论（IIT）对有意识加工的时空动力学提出了不同的预测。Cogitate联盟（2025）最近进行了一项大规模对抗性合作，以在GNWT与IIT之间做出仲裁；然而，他们的一些分析得出了混杂的发现。由于原始研究依赖功能连接，无法评估定向神经影响，从而无法直接检验这些理论的机制性主张。在此，我们使用动态因果模型（DCM）重新分析了Cogitate联盟的脑磁图（MEG）数据集（N = 100），以检验每种理论在任务无关面孔知觉期间预测的有效连接模式。我们采用了分割发现-验证程序和扩展时间窗口方法来追踪每种理论的原型网络的时间演化。具体来说，我们通过检验刺激开始和结束时前额叶（PFC）向类别选择性区域的反馈的必要性，评估了GNWT关于阶段性前额叶点燃的预测，然后通过检验内容特异性区域内的外部反馈和内在连接，检验了IIT关于持续性后部整合的预测。我们发现GNWT在刺激开始和结束时关于前额叶点燃的两个预测都得到了支持；值得注意的是，后者在原始的Cogitate分析中未曾观察到。对于IIT，虽然梭状回与初级视觉皮层之间的反馈连接并未在整个刺激持续期间保持，但梭状回的内在连接与IIT关于在内容特异性基质内持续性加工的预测大体一致。然而，一项比较面孔和物体特异性DCM网络的事后验证分析在任何时间窗口都没有提供面孔选择性的强证据（pp < 0.95），因此尤其需要对IIT的发现持保守解释。

## Abstract
The neural mechanisms underlying conscious perception remain contested, with global neuronal workspace theory (GNWT) and integrated information theory (IIT) offering divergent predictions about the spatiotemporal dynamics of conscious processing. The Cogitate Consortium (2025) recently conducted a large-scale adversarial collaboration to arbitrate between GNWT and IIT; however, some of their analyses yielded mixed findings. As the original study relied on functional connectivity, it could not assess the directed neural influences required to directly test the mechanistic claims of these theories. Here, we re-examined the Cogitate Consortium MEG dataset (N = 100) using dynamic causal modelling (DCM) to test the effective connectivity profiles predicted by each theory during task-irrelevant face perception. We adopted a split discovery-validation procedure and an expanding time-window approach to track the temporal evolution of prototypical networks for each theory. Specifically, we evaluated GNWTs prediction of phasic prefrontal ignition by testing the necessity of PFC feedback to category-selective areas at stimulus onset and offset, and then tested IITs prediction of sustained posterior integration by testing extrinsic feedback and intrinsic connectivity within content-specific regions. We found support for both GNWT predictions of prefrontal ignition at stimulus onset and offset; noting that the latter was not previously observed in the original Cogitate analyses. For IIT, whilst feedback connectivity between the fusiform gyrus and primary visual cortex was not sustained throughout the stimulus duration, intrinsic fusiform gyrus connectivity was broadly consistent with IITs prediction of sustained processing within a content-specific substrate. However, a post-hoc validation analysis comparing face- and object-specific DCM networks did not yield strong evidence for face selectivity at any time window (pp< 0.95), warranting a conservative interpretation of the IIT findings in particular.
