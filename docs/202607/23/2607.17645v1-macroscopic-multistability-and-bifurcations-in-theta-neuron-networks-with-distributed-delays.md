## 摘要
我们研究一个具有突触相互作用的完全相同theta神经元全连接网络，其中突触相互作用由分布时间延迟介导。利用Watanabe-Strogatz降维并在运动常数均匀分布的假设下取热力学极限，我们导出了复序参量的单个延迟微分方程。延迟由一组具有指定平均延迟的延迟核建模，允许在统一框架内处理离散和分布延迟。降维系统的平衡点可分为两个几何上不同的族：单位圆上的类型1平衡点和实轴上的类型2平衡点。对于这两族，局部稳定性问题归结为涉及延迟核的Laplace-Stieltjes变换的标量特征方程。我们获得了可容许核的稳定性准则，并给出了Dirac核的显式Hopf分支条件，并与弱和强Gamma核进行了额外比较。结果表明，延迟可能保持稳定性、使平衡点失稳或产生稳定性切换，这取决于平衡分支、参数区域和核的选择。离散延迟情况的数值模拟支持了分析结果，并展示了相应的相图、吸引域、吸引子共存以及延迟诱导的周期动力学。

## Abstract
We study an all-to-all coupled network of identical theta neurons with synaptic interaction mediated by a distributed time delay. Using the Watanabe-Strogatz reduction and passing to the thermodynamic limit under the assumption of uniformly distributed constants of motion, we derive a single delay differential equation for the complex order parameter. The delay is modeled by a family of delay kernels with prescribed mean delay, allowing discrete and distributed delays to be treated in a unified framework. The equilibria of the reduced system can be classified into two geometrically distinct families: type 1 equilibria on the unit circle and type 2 equilibria on the real axis. For both families, the local stability problem reduces to scalar characteristic equations involving the Laplace-Stieltjes transform of the delay kernel. We obtain stability criteria for admissible kernels and explicit Hopf bifurcation conditions for the Dirac kernel, with additional comparison to weak and strong Gamma kernels. The results show that the delay may either preserve stability, destabilize equilibria, or produce stability switching, depending on the equilibrium branch, parameter regime, and choice of kernel. Numerical simulations for the discrete-delay case support the analytical results and illustrate the corresponding phase portraits, basins of attraction, coexistence of attractors, and delay-induced periodic dynamics.
