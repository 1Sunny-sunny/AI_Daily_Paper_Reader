## 摘要
跨物种的头部方向系统中呈现了朝向信息，包括果蝇。然而，对于导航决策如何做出以及目标记忆如何表征，特别是在休息期间，人们知之甚少。在此，我们使用一种适用于在虚拟现实中行走的果蝇的导航学习分析，描述了方向选择和记忆的神经动力学。扇形体内与导航和记忆相关的神经元在动物休息时持续漂移，相对于行走方向偏移了180°。休息期间的光遗传激活偏向后续的行走方向。学习导致休息期间漂移的变化，揭示了目标方向的记忆。下游神经元纠正180°的偏移，因此在休息期间重新激活行走方向。连接组揭示了一个紧凑的用于重新激活的电路架构，并通过计算模型进行了验证。因此，漂移驱动行为，在休息期间重新激活行走方向，并被记忆塑造，表明跨物种导航回路中记忆巩固的相似性。

## Abstract
Heading information is represented in head direction systems across species, including Drosophila. However, how navigation decisions are made and how goal memories are represented, particularly during rest, is little understood. Here, using a navigation learning assay for flies walking in virtual reality, we describe neural dynamics for direction selection and memory. Neurons in the fan-shaped body, a navigation and memory related structure, continually drift when the animal is at rest, shifted by 180{whitebullet} compared to walking directions. Optogenetic activation during rest biases subsequent walking direction. Learning leads to changes in drift during rest, revealing a memory of goal direction. Downstream neurons correct the 180{whitebullet} shift, therefore reactivating walking directions during rest. The connectome reveals a compact circuit architecture for reactivation, as validated using computational modeling. Thus, drift drives behavior, reactivates walking directions during rest, and is shaped by memory, suggesting similarities for memory consolidation in navigation circuits across species.
