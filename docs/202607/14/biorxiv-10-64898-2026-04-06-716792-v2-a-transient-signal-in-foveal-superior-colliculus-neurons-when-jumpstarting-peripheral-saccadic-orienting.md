## 摘要
上丘（SC）既能感知环境，也能在其中定向凝视。尽管SC的感觉性和运动性爆发在性质上彼此相似，但两种处理机制（视觉和运动）中的群体活动结构却大相径庭，因此需要一种迄今尚未探索的快速表征转换，这种转换仅在数十毫秒的时间尺度上发生。这里，我们首先表明，当计划中的扫视通过启动信号释放时，代表扫视目标位置的外周SC神经元在其运动爆发最终出现之前，会表现出瞬时的、短潜伏期的停顿。该停顿在启动信号后的约50毫秒内开始；它是刺激依赖性的；并且纯视觉SC神经元中的停顿比扫视相关神经元中的显著更弱。另一方面，中央凹SC神经元则瞬时爆发，其爆发领先外周神经元的停顿约10毫秒。这些瞬时中央凹爆发也领先于同一中央凹神经元与扫视相关的停顿，后者与运动生成时间锁定。值得注意的是，在即时的视觉引导扫视任务中，需要在不到50-100毫秒内完成从视觉到运动外周爆发的转换，瞬时中央凹SC爆发仍然出现，导致在两个不同的SC位点同时出现短潜伏期爆发：一个在中央凹；另一个在偏心位置并对扫视目标的视觉出现做出反应。我们的结果表明，在用于研究灵长类大脑视觉、运动和认知过程的经典扫视任务中，瞬时中央凹SC信号先于外周扫视定向，并可能促进SC扫视运动爆发所需的一种必要的快速表征转换。

## Abstract
The superior colliculus (SC) both senses the environment and orients gaze within it. While the SC's sensory and motor bursts appear qualitatively similar to each other, population activity structure in the two processing regimes (visual and motor) is very different, necessitating a hitherto unexplored rapid representational transformation, occurring on the scale of only tens of milliseconds. Here, we first show that when a planned saccade is released with a go signal, peripheral SC neurons representing the saccade target location exhibit a transient, short-latency pause right before their motor bursts eventually emerge. This pause starts within ~50 ms from the go signal; it is stimulus-dependent; and it is significantly weaker in purely visual SC neurons than in saccade-related ones. Foveal SC neurons, on the other hand, transiently burst, and their bursts lead the peripheral neurons' pauses by ~10 milliseconds. These transient foveal bursts also lead the same foveal neurons' saccade-related pauses, which are time-locked to movement generation. Remarkably, during immediate visually-guided saccade tasks, requiring a transformation from visual to motor peripheral bursts in <50-100 ms, the transient foveal SC bursts still occur, resulting in simultaneous short-latency bursting at two disparate SC loci: one foveal; and one eccentric and responding to the visual appearance of the saccade target. Our results suggest that in classic saccade tasks used to investigate visual, motor, and cognitive processes in primate brains, a transient foveal SC signal precedes peripheral saccadic orienting, and may facilitate a necessary rapid representational transformation needed for SC saccade motor bursts to ensue.
