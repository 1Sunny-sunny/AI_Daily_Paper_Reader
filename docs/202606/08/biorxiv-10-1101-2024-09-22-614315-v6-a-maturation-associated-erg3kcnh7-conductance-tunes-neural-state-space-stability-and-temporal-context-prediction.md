## 摘要
神经成熟必须在保持灵活的状态动态的同时，防止这些动态变得不稳定、难以解读或与学习不匹配。在此，我将编码ERG3/Kv11.3钾通道的KCNH7确定为发育相关的此类运行模式的候选调节因子。公开的发育期、单细胞和Patch-seq分析将KCNH7/ERG3与出生后后期的表达和被动态电生理轴联系起来。在基于电导的浦肯野细胞模型中，类似ERG3/KCNH7的电导提高了强直放电的阈值，同时拓宽了强直前反应类别并增强了靶标特异性的节律募集，这表明了一种运行模式转换而非简单的兴奋性刹车。受KCNH7启发的自适应动力学稳定了脉冲学习，并重塑了超出标量阈值或温度控制的高负荷联想检索。在循环电导库中，ERG3/KCNH7的尺度和半激活电压调节了驱动动态模式、脉冲态与电压态记忆以及依赖于时序上下文的预测。在受损的状态传输下，适度的野生型样ERG3/KCNH7电导将脆弱的高动态基线转换为低保真支持模式。公开的体内群体和行为分析进一步支持了以下观点：发育和疾病相关表型可以解释为运行模式控制的改变，而非简单的活动或感觉表现变化。这些结果支持将KCNH7/ERG3作为连接离子通道成熟与稳定且灵活的神经计算的模型案例。

## Abstract
Neural maturation must preserve flexible state dynamics while preventing those dynamics from becoming unstable, unreadable, or mismatched to learning. Here, I identify KCNH7, encoding the ERG3/Kv11.3 potassium channel, as a maturation-associated candidate regulator of such operating regimes. Public developmental, single-cell, and Patch-seq analyses linked KCNH7/ERG3 to late postnatal expression and passive-state electrophysiological axes. In conductance-based Purkinje-cell models, ERG3/KCNH7-like conductance increased the threshold for tonic firing while broadening pre-tonic response classes and enhancing target-specific rhythmic recruitment, indicating a regime shift rather than a simple excitability brake. KCNH7-inspired adaptive dynamics stabilized spiking learning and reshaped high-load associative retrieval beyond scalar threshold or temperature control. In recurrent conductance reservoirs, ERG3/KCNH7 scale and half-activation voltage tuned driven dynamical regime, spike-state versus voltage-state memory, and temporal-context-dependent prediction. Under degraded state transmission, moderate WT-like ERG3/KCNH7 conductance converted a vulnerable high-dynamic baseline into a low-fidelity support regime. Public in vivo population and behavioral analyses further supported the view that maturation- and disease-related phenotypes can be interpreted as alterations in operating-regime control rather than simple changes in activity or sensory performance. These results support KCNH7/ERG3 as a model case for linking ion-channel maturation to stable yet flexible neural computation.
