## 摘要
韵律是言语的一个基本方面，以音高等超音段特征为特点。韵律音高轮廓用于传达说话者的意图，例如陈述或提问。理解这些意图需要将连续、可变的音高信息抽象为离散范畴。范畴感知被认为以效应器特异的方式募集运动系统，其中控制运动效应器的皮层区域通过识别发音姿势来支持语音识别。然而，参与音调产生的效应器是否同样对韵律范畴感知做出贡献仍不清楚。为解决这一问题，我们收集了29名参与者（15名女性）的脑磁图数据，他们首先演唱由五音旋律排列的音调，然后识别单个词的韵律（陈述 vs. 疑问），这些词的音高轮廓沿五级连续体变化。采用区域限制的探照灯方法从静息态解码演唱，我们定位了两个音调产生的运动前区，对应于腹侧和背侧喉部运动皮层。独立的神经解码分析显示，在这些区域，尤其是在与音高调节更密切相关的背侧喉部运动皮层，感知的韵律范畴是可解码的。重要的是，解码表现反映了整个连续体上韵律范畴的行为可辨别性，表明这些区域参与感知决策。最后，音调运动区与听觉区域交换了范畴相关信息，表明这些区域不仅仅是回声般地处理听觉区域的信息。这些发现共同突显了效应器特异的运动支持对韵律范畴感知的作用，从而拓宽了我们对运动参与言语感知的理解。

意义陈述 言语感知被认为会募集运动前皮层，不同的子区域将言语声音与产生它们所用的发音姿势联系起来。我们通过韵律——即言语中传达陈述和疑问等含义的音高变化——来研究这一观点。使用脑磁图，在演唱任务中识别出音调运动区，并检验它们是否表征感知到的韵律范畴。我们发现，在这些区域中韵律范畴是可区分的，并且这种神经可辨别性与整个清晰和模糊韵律连续体上的行为可辨别性一致，提示其参与了感知决策。这些发现不太可能反映来自听觉区域的被动回声，因为在范畴处理过程中，音调运动区主动影响了它们。我们的结果突显了效应器特异的运动支持在形成抽象韵律表征中的作用。

## Abstract
Prosody is a fundamental aspect of speech characterized by suprasegmental features such as pitch. Prosodic pitch contours are used to convey speakers intentions, for example, to make a statement or ask a question. Understanding these intentions requires abstracting continuous, variable pitch information into discrete categories. Category perception has been proposed to recruit the motor system in an effector-specific manner, whereby cortical areas controlling motor effectors support speech sound recognition by identifying articulatory gestures. However, it remains unclear whether effectors involved in pitch production similarly contribute to prosodic category perception. To address this question, we collected magnetoencephalography data from 29 participants (15 females) while they first sang pitches arranged in five-tone melodies and then identified the prosody (Statement vs. Question) of single words varying in pitch contour along a five-level continuum. Using a region-restricted searchlight approach to decode singing from rest, we localized two premotor regions for pitch production, corresponding to the ventral and dorsal laryngeal motor cortex (LMC). A separate neural decoding analysis revealed that perceived prosodic categories were decodable in these regions, especially from the dorsal LMC that is more closely associated with pitch regulation. Importantly, decoding performance mirrored behavioral discriminability of prosodic categories across the continuum, suggesting that these regions are involved in perceptual decision-making. Finally, pitch motor areas exchanged category-related information with auditory regions, indicating these areas do not merely echo the processing in auditory regions. Together, these findings highlight effector-specific motor support for prosodic category perception, thereby broadening our understanding of motor involvement in speech perception.

Significance StatementSpeech perception has been proposed to recruit the premotor cortex, with different subregions linking speech sounds to the articulatory gestures used to produce them. We investigated this idea through prosody--pitch changes in speech conveying meanings such as statements and questions. Using magnetoencephalography, we identified pitch motor areas during a singing task and tested whether they represent perceived prosodic categories. We found that prosodic categories were distinguishable in these regions and that this neural discriminability mirrored behavioral discriminability across clear and ambiguous prosody, suggesting involvement in perceptual decision-making. These findings are unlikely to reflect passive echoes from auditory regions, as pitch motor areas actively influenced them during categorical processing. Our results highlight effector-specific motor support for forming abstract prosodic representations.
