## 摘要
视觉加工传统上使用静态观看范式进行研究，其中研究者分析大脑对一系列随机选择刺激起始的反应。将这种“刺激起始”方法转化为允许自由眼动的主动视觉范式时，研究者通常将注视起始视为触发整个视觉系统视觉活动的事件。在此，我们通过分析一个大规模脑磁图（MEG）数据集来检验这一假设，该数据集同步记录了5名自由探索数千张自然图像的参与者的眼动，产生了约200,000个凝视事件。我们表明，与扫视相关的事件，特别是扫视曲率峰值，而非注视起始，解释了早期感觉成分M100潜伏期的大部分变异。此外，比较由刺激起始引发的经典M100与主动视觉中引发的M100，揭示了两者在响应扫视相关事件和注视起始事件时的显著差异。这表明，使用黄金标准刺激起始范式发现的现象并不一定能推广到自然视觉。我们的发现挑战了在静态范式中研究视觉的普遍方法，并强调了内部生成和行动驱动的信号在自然感觉加工动力学中的重要性。

## Abstract
Visual processing is traditionally studied using static viewing paradigms in which researchers analyse brain responses to the onsets of a sequence of randomly selected stimuli. Translating this 'stimulus onset' approach to active vision paradigms that allow for free eye-movements, researchers often consider fixation onsets as the events that trigger visual activity across the visual system. Here, we test this assumption by analysing a large-scale magnetoencephalography (MEG) dataset with simultaneously recorded eye movements of 5 participants who freely explored thousands of natural images, yielding approximately 200,000 gaze events. We show that saccade-related events, particularly peak saccade curvature, rather than fixation onsets, explain most variance in latency of the early sensory component M100. Further, comparing the classic M100 elicited by stimulus onsets with the M100s elicited during active vision revealed stark differences, both in response to saccade-related and fixation-onset events. This indicates that phenomena discovered using gold standard stimulus onset paradigms do not necessarily translate to natural vision. Our findings challenge the prevailing approach to studying vision in static paradigms and highlight the importance of internally generated and action-driven signals in the dynamics of natural sensory processing.
