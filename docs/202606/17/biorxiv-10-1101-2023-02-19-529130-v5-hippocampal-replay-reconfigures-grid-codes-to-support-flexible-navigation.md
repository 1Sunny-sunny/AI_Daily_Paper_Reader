## 摘要
大脑的认知地图依赖于内嗅皮层网格细胞，其六边形放电模式为空间和抽象知识提供了度量标准，同时也依赖于海马回放，即对经验进行压缩再激活以支持学习和规划。海马回放是否参与内嗅皮层网格度量的组织尚未明确。在此，我们记录了一项方向分辨导航任务期间的脑磁图数据，该任务为每位参与者产生了1,800个试次独特事件，从而能够在整个360度范围内按事件估计回放和内嗅皮层网格样活动。在每个导航事件早期出现了反向和正向回放，随后出现六重内嗅皮层网格编码。回放锚定于网格轴，其中反向回放时间锁定于网格编码窗口早期表达的网格取向，而正向回放锁定于后期表达的网格取向。值得注意的是，正向和反向回放分别编码了每个网格轴的两个相反方向（相隔180度），共同覆盖了完整的六重结构。一个连续吸引子网络重现了这一组织，网格轴回放产生的网格度显著高于无回放的模拟。最后，导航行为验证了这些发现。在网格样活动出现前，移动速度表现出双重方向周期性，峰值位于两个相反方向（相隔180度），与正向和反向回放的方向选择性镜像对应；而轨迹偏离在网格样活动出现后表现出双重周期性，并与网格取向对齐，表明网格取向旋转以引导朝向目标的行进方向。

## Abstract
The brain's cognitive map relies on entorhinal grid cells, whose hexagonal firing provides a metric for spatial and abstract knowledge, and on hippocampal replay, the compressed reactivation of experience that supports learning and planning. Whether hippocampal replay contributes to the organization of the entorhinal grid metric remains unresolved. Here we recorded magnetoencephalography during a direction-resolved navigation task that yielded 1,800 trial-unique events per participant, allowing replay and entorhinal grid-like activity to be estimated event-wise across the full 360{degrees}. Reverse and forward replay emerged early in each navigation event, followed by a six-fold entorhinal grid code. Replay was anchored to the grid axes, with reverse replay time-locked to the grid orientation expressed early in the grid-coding window and forward replay to that expressed later. Notably, forward and reverse replay encoded the two opposite directions (180{degrees} apart) of each grid axis, together spanning the full six-fold structure. A continuous-attractor network reproduced this organization, with grid-axis replay yielding significantly higher gridness than simulations without replay. Finally, navigation behaviour corroborated these findings. Movement speed showed two-fold directional periodicity before grid-like activity emerged, peaking at two opposite directions (180{degrees}apart) that mirrored the opposite tuning of forward and reverse replay, whereas trajectory deviation showed two-fold periodicity after grid-like activity emerged and aligned with the grid orientation, suggesting that the grid orientation rotates to guide heading toward the goal.
