## 摘要
躯体感觉处理的主流模型认为，触觉和本体感觉线索的整合对于触觉感知至关重要。然而，这种整合并非总是有益的。虽然依赖于姿势的触觉信号重映射对于引导手部动作至关重要，但当触觉独立于手部姿势表征时，对某些触觉特征的辨别会得到增强。大脑如何根据任务需求灵活控制触觉和本体感觉线索的整合仍不清楚，因为大多数研究只在单一参考系内考察了触觉感知。在此，我们研究了在改变手部姿势的情况下，参考系需求如何塑造人类在手指上进行运动判断时的触觉感知。参与者被提示报告相对于手指或胸骨（即身体中线）的运动方向。我们发现，触觉和本体感觉线索以任务特定的方式整合，手部姿势只在以胸骨为中心的任务中使判断产生偏差。此外，我们观察到以胸骨为中心的判断反应时间更快，累积到边界模型表明这一优势是由较低的决策阈值驱动的。最后，我们发展了一个贝叶斯计算框架，形式化了触觉运动信号如何在不同手部姿势间转换，推进了我们对大脑如何将感觉信息从皮肤坐标系映射到身体中心坐标系的理解。总之，这些发现表明，在任务依赖性坐标变换的贝叶斯计算框架内，参考系任务需求塑造了触觉信息的转换和知觉决策的速度。

## Abstract
Leading models of somatosensory processing posit that integration of tactile and proprioceptive cues is essential for touch perception. Yet, such integration is not universally beneficial. While posture-dependent remapping of tactile signals is critical for guiding hand actions, discrimination of certain tactile features is enhanced when touch is represented independently of hand posture. How the brain flexibly controls the integration of tactile and proprioceptive cues based on task demands remains unclear, as most studies have only examined touch perception within a single reference frame. Here, we studied how reference frame demands shape touch perception in humans making motion judgements on a finger, while varying hand posture. Participants were cued to report motion direction relative to the finger or the sternum (i.e., the midline of the body). We found that tactile and proprioceptive cues are integrated in a task-specific manner, with hand posture biasing judgments only in the Sternum-centric task. Further, we observe that reaction times are faster for Sternum-centric judgments, with accumulation-to-bound models indicating that this advantage is driven by lower decision thresholds. Finally, we developed a Bayesian computational framework that formalizes how tactile motion signals are transformed across hand postures, advancing our understanding of how the brain maps sensory information from skin- to body-centered coordinates. Together, these findings demonstrate that reference frame task demands shape both the transformation of tactile information and speed of perceptual decisions, within a Bayesian computational framework for task-dependent coordinate transformations.
