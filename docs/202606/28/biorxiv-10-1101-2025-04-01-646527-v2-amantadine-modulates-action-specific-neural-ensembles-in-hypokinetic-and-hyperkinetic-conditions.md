## 摘要
背景：在基底节环路的经典模型中，直接和间接通路中的纹状体棘状投射神经元（dSPNs、iSPNs）分别促进和抑制运动，并在运动减少或运动过多状态下表现出不平衡的活动水平。针对这些状态的大多数疗法被认为是通过重新平衡 dSPNs 和 iSPNs 的相对活动至正常水平而发挥作用。然而，金刚烷胺既能改善运动减少又能改善运动过多状态，其作用机制尚不清楚。

目的：确定金刚烷胺是通过正常化 dSPN 和 iSPN 活动平衡，还是通过一种独特的机制来恢复运动功能。

方法：我们在帕金森病的6-OHDA小鼠模型中使用双色双光子钙成像技术，同时监测健康、运动减少（帕金森样）和运动过多（异动症样）状态下 dSPN 和 iSPN 的动态变化。

结果：我们评估了背外侧纹状体中 dSPN/iSPN 活动平衡以及动作特异性神经集群活动。在运动减少状态下，左旋多巴挽救了 dSPN/iSPN 失衡，但未能恢复被破坏的运动特异性集群活动。相反，金刚烷胺改善了运动特异性集群活动，但并未正常化 dSPN/iSPN 失衡。在运动过多状态下，前肢异动症表现为与编码运动的神经活动模式不同的特征。金刚烷胺选择性抑制了前肢异动症集群的静息活动，而不影响运动编码集群或恢复通路平衡。

结论：左旋多巴和金刚烷胺可能通过不同机制发挥作用，左旋多巴使通路平衡正常化，而金刚烷胺调节动作特异性神经集群。这些发现支持了在运动减少和运动过多状态下动作编码紊乱的重要性，并提示纠正这些紊乱可以恢复运动功能。

## Abstract
BackgroundIn the classical model of basal ganglia circuitry, striatal spiny projection neurons of the direct and indirect pathways (dSPNs, iSPNs) promote and suppress movement, respectively, and exhibit unbalanced activity levels during hypokinetic or hyperkinetic conditions. Most therapies for these conditions are thought to work by rebalancing the relative activity of dSPNs and iSPNs towards normal levels. However, the mechanism of amantadine, which uniquely improves both hypokinetic and hyperkinetic conditions, is poorly understood.

ObjectiveTo determine whether amantadine restores motor function by normalizing the balance of dSPN and iSPN activity or through a distinct mechanism.

MethodsWe used dual-color two-photon Ca2+ imaging in the 6-OHDA mouse model of Parkinsons disease to concurrently monitor dSPN and iSPN dynamics across healthy, hypokinetic (parkinsonian), and hyperkinetic (dyskinetic) conditions.

ResultsWe evaluated both dSPN/iSPN activity balance and action-specific neural ensemble activity in the dorsolateral striatum. In hypokinetic conditions, L-DOPA rescued the dSPN/iSPN imbalance but failed to restore the disrupted activity of the locomotion-specific ensemble. Conversely, amantadine improved locomotion-specific ensemble activity without normalizing the dSPN/iSPN imbalance. In hyperkinetic conditions, forelimb dyskinesias were characterized by neural activity patterns distinct from those encoding locomotion. Amantadine selectively suppressed the resting activity of forelimb dyskinesia ensembles without affecting locomotion-coding ensembles or restoring pathway balance.

ConclusionsL-DOPA and amantadine may act through distinct mechanisms, with L-DOPA normalizing pathway balance and amantadine modulating action-specific neural ensembles. These findings support the importance of action-coding disruptions during hypokinetic and hyperkinetic conditions and suggest that correcting them can restore motor function.
