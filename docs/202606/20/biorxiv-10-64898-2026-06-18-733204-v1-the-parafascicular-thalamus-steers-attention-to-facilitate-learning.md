## 摘要
能预测奖励的线索可以获得强大的导向和趋近行为偏向能力，引导寻求行为接近奖励。旁束旁丘脑（PF）是板内核复合体的一部分，作为整合皮层下感觉和注意信号以产生支持线索引导行为的动作的神经中枢，其位置十分合适。PF中的神经元对感觉线索做出反应并编码头部位置的特征，但人们对PF在体内学习过程中如何被激活知之甚少。在此，我们使用光纤光度测量法在巴甫洛夫条件作用任务全程记录了PF神经元的钙活动。我们发现PF神经元发展出持续的线索诱发反应，该反应随联想学习而增强，随消退而减弱。PF神经元优先传递线索导向的定向行为和在线索期间做出的身体动作的信号，并且在奖赏消耗期间其活动暂停。利用光遗传学，我们发现刺激PF神经元通过促进夸大的同侧转向行为来扰乱正常的线索导向定向，并损害联想学习。总的来说，这些结果表明了一种编码特征，PF可以通过动态调节头部和身体位置来支持线索和奖赏的趋近行为。综上所述，我们的数据证明了一个在引导注意以促进线索-奖赏学习中起重要作用的丘脑区域。

## Abstract
Cues that predict reward can acquire strong power to bias orientation and approach, directing seeking behaviors into close proximity with reward. The parafascicular thalamus (PF), part of the intralaminar complex, is well-placed as a neural hub for integrating subcortical sensory and attentional signals to generate actions that support cue-guided behavior. Neurons in the PF respond to sensory cues and encode features of head position, but little is known about how the PF is engaged in vivo during learning. Here, we recorded calcium activity in PF neurons using fiber photometry throughout a pavlovian conditioning task. We found that PF neurons developed sustained cue-evoked responses that scaled with associative learning and diminished with extinction. PF neurons preferentially signaled cue-directed orientations and body movements made during the cue, and their activity paused during reward consumption. Using optogenetics, we found that stimulation of PF neurons disrupted normal cue-directed orientation and impaired associative learning by promoting exaggerated ipsilateral turning behavior. Broadly, these results suggest an encoding profile by which the PF can support cue and reward approach behavior via dynamic regulation of head and body position. Together, our data demonstrate a thalamic region that is important for steering attention to facilitate cue-reward learning.
