## 摘要
视觉处理需要任务相关信息在皮层层次结构间的灵活路由。一种提出的机制是嵌套振荡活动，其中低频节律动态调节局部兴奋性和区域间通信。然而，该框架的具体预测尚未在主动刺激加工过程中，于精细空间和时间尺度上进行检验。在此，我们重新分析了在猕猴执行图形-背景分离任务时从V1和V4记录的局部场电位和多单元活动。我们发现，V1中的阿尔法活动在刺激后短暂的窗口期内携带关于图形位置和刺激朝向的信息，并具有精细的空间特异性，该窗口期与图形-背景调制的出现和主要的V4至V1反馈同时发生。在此窗口期内，局部放电活动和V1与V4间的区域耦合均依赖于阿尔法幅度和瞬时V1-V4相位差，表明低频同步通过协调调节局部兴奋性和层级反馈交互来塑造皮层群体间的有效通信。总之，这些发现支持了阿尔法频段活动通过协调调节局部兴奋性和层级反馈交互，反映视觉处理过程中精细尺度信息路由的观点。

## Abstract
Visual processing requires flexible routing of task-relevant information across cortical hierarchies. One proposed mechanism is nested oscillatory activity, in which low-frequency rhythms dynamically modulate local excitability and inter-areal communication. However, the specific predictions of this framework have not been tested during active stimulus processing at fine spatial and temporal scales. Here, we reanalyzed local field potentials (LFPs) and multi-unit activity (MUA) recorded from V1 and V4 in macaque monkeys performing a figure--ground segregation task. We show that alpha activity in V1 carries information about the position of the figure and the orientation of the stimulus with fine spatial specificity within a transient post-stimulus window, a period that coincides with the emergence of figure-ground modulation and the dominant V4[-&gt;]V1 feedback. During this window, both local spiking activity and inter-areal coupling between V1 and V4 depended on alpha amplitude and on the instantaneous V1-V4 phase difference, indicating that low-frequency synchronization shapes effective communication between cortical populations. Together, these findings support the view that alpha-band activity reflect fine-scale information routing during visual processing through coordinated modulation of local excitability and hierarchical feedback interactions.
