## 摘要
人类大脑在空间上沿功能梯度组织，但这些梯度的时间组织仍知之甚少。在脑电图和脑磁图中，由于容积传导掩盖了时间优先性，这一问题一直难以解决。本文引入滞后特异性正交化，这是对成对正交化的扩展，能够估计非零滞后的幅度包络相关性。将此方法应用于大规模静息态脑磁图数据，我们识别出电生理滞后线程：可重复的时间顺序图谱，在alpha和beta频段最为相干，并将活动从枕叶和感觉运动区组织到额叶和颞叶联合皮层。这些快速电生理滞后线程与慢得多的fMRI滞后线程共享个体特异性的空间结构，支持fMRI滞后结构源于神经活动而非血管效应。最后，我们表明皮层滞后线程并非固定的解剖约束，它们随年龄和利手性共变，并在认知任务执行过程中被选择性重构。这些发现共同揭示了人类皮层频率特异性的电生理时间层级，将快速神经动态与较慢的血液动力学传播结构联系起来。

## Abstract
The human brain is spatially organized along functional gradients, but the temporal organization of these gradients remains poorly understood. In EEG and MEG, this question has been difficult to address because volume conduction obscures temporal precedence. Here, we introduce lag-specific orthogonalization, an extension of pairwise orthogonalization that allows us to estimate non-zero-lag amplitude-envelope correlations. Applying this approach to large-scale resting-state MEG, we identify electrophysiological lag threads: reproducible maps of temporal ordering that are most coherent in the alpha and beta bands and organize activity from occipital and sensorimotor regions toward frontal and temporal association cortex. These fast electrophysiological lag threads share subject-specific spatial structure with much slower fMRI lag threads, supporting a neural rather than vascular origin of fMRI lag structure. Finally, we show that cortical lag threads are not fixed anatomical constraints. They covary with age and handedness and are selectively reconfigured during cognitive task performance. Together, these findings reveal a frequency-specific electrophysiological temporal hierarchy of the human cortex that links fast neural dynamics to slower hemodynamic propagation structure.
