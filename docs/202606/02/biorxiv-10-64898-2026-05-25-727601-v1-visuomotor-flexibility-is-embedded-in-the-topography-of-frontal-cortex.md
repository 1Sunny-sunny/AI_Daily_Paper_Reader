## 摘要
视觉选择和眼跳目标之间并非强制耦合，一个位置的刺激可以引导眼球运动至另一个位置。这一过程由额叶动眼皮层介导，其中视觉和运动空间的地图汇聚，但这些地图之间的地形关系是否本身促成了这种灵活性尚不清楚。利用Neuropixels记录穿越狨猴皮层横截面的活动，我们发现单个视觉和眼跳矢量角度平滑变化，偶有突然跳跃。局部皮层区域并非呈现一对一的视觉-运动映射，而是包含对齐和错位矢量角度的组合。马赛克地图模型再现了视觉和运动地图局部角度差异的经验分布，揭示出这两个地图具有约束且截然不同的空间尺度。当这些马赛克地图组合时，生成了一种莫尔条纹图案，定量匹配了观察到的视觉-运动角度差异，为视觉输入与眼跳输出之间的灵活转换提供了基础。

## Abstract
Visual selection and saccade targeting are not obligatorily coupled, allowing a stimulus at one location to guide an eye movement toward another. This is mediated by frontal oculomotor cortex, where maps of visual and motor space converge, yet whether the topographic relationship between these maps itself contributes to this flexibility is unknown. Using Neuropixels recordings across horizontal segments of marmoset cortex, we find that individually visual and saccade vector angles change smoothly, with occasional abrupt jumps. Rather than a one-to-one visual-motor mapping, local cortical patches contain a combination of aligned and misaligned vector angles. Mosaic map models reproduce the empirical distributions of local angle differences for visual and motor maps, revealing that the two maps have constrained and distinct spatial scales. When combined, these mosaic maps generate a moire pattern that quantitatively matches the observed visual-motor angular differences, providing a substrate for flexible transformations between visual inputs and saccade outputs.
