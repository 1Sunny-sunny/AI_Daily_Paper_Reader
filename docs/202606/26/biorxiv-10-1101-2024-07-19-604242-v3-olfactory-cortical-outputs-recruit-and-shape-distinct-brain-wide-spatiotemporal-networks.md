## 摘要
气味信息从嗅球并行传递至多个初级嗅觉皮层区域，包括前嗅核（AON）和梨状皮层（Pir）。然而，由于缺乏合适的在体技术，嗅球和皮层输出在与全脑其他互连区域更广泛相互作用中的具体作用尚不清楚。此外，嗅觉相关功能障碍与神经系统疾病之间新出现的关联，凸显了在系统层面研究嗅觉网络的必要性。利用光遗传学、功能磁共振成像和计算建模，我们探究了嗅觉网络中全脑神经相互作用的时空特性。我们观察到不同的下游招募模式。具体而言，刺激嗅球中的兴奋性投射神经元主要激活初级嗅觉网络区域，而刺激AON和Pir中的嗅球传入纤维则分别主要顺向激活海马/纹状体和边缘网络。在时间上，与Pir刺激相比，重复的嗅球或AON刺激会减弱全脑神经活动的传播。动态因果模型分析揭示了AON输出对纹状体和边缘网络区域具有强大的抑制作用。此外，在衰老大鼠模型中的实验显示，嗅球刺激后的全脑激活降低，特别是在初级嗅觉和边缘网络中。建模分析发现AON到Pir的连接功能失调，表明这一初级嗅觉皮层环路受损，进而阻碍了下游长程传播。我们的研究首次描绘了嗅觉神经活动在全脑网络中传播的时空特性，并区分了初级嗅觉皮层输出（AON和Pir）在系统层面塑造神经相互作用中的角色。

## Abstract
Odor information is transmitted from the olfactory bulb to several primary olfactory cortical regions in parallel, including the anterior olfactory nucleus (AON) and piriform cortex (Pir). However, the specific roles of the olfactory bulb and cortical outputs in wider interactions with other interconnected regions throughout the brain remain unclear due to the lack of suitable in vivo techniques. Furthermore, emerging associations between olfactory-related dysfunctions and neurological disorders underscore the need for examining olfactory networks at the systems level. Using optogenetics, fMRI, and computational modeling, we interrogated the spatiotemporal properties of brain-wide neural interactions in olfactory networks. We observed distinct downstream recruitment patterns. Specifically, stimulation of excitatory projection neurons in OB predominantly activates primary olfactory network regions, while stimulation of OB afferents in AON and Pir primarily orthodromically activates hippocampal/striatal and limbic networks, respectively. Temporally, repeated OB or AON stimulation diminishes neural activity propagation brain-wide in contrast to Pir stimulation. Dynamic causal modeling analysis reveals a robust inhibitory effect of AON outputs on striatal and limbic network regions. In addition, experiments in aged rat models show decreased brain-wide activation following OB stimulation, particularly in the primary olfactory and limbic networks. Modeling analysis identifies a dysfunctional AON to Pir connection, indicating the impairment of this primary olfactory cortical circuit that disrupts the downstream long-range propagation. Our study delineates the spatiotemporal properties of olfactory neural activity propagation in brain-wide networks for the first time and distinguishes the roles of primary olfactory cortical, AON and Pir, outputs in shaping neural interactions at the systems level.
