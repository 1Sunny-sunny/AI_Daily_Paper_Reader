## 摘要
相位-幅度耦合（PAC）被广泛用于量化不同时间尺度上神经振荡之间的相互作用，并常被解释为反映了慢速和快速神经活动之间在时间上有意义的协调。结合经验脑电图数据和数学分析，我们表明，当低频振荡的相位反转180°时，广泛使用的PAC度量——调制指数（MI）保持不变。相反，偏好相位移恰好旋转180°，符合预期。因此，交叉频率耦合的相反时间组织可以产生相同的MI值。这一发现定义了基于MI的PAC的一个基本解释局限性：MI量化了相位依赖的幅度调制强度，但不编码其时间极性。因此，单独的MI无法区分时间对齐和时间反转的耦合配置，也无法支持关于时间对齐、相位极性或方向性的推断。由于PAC在神经科学中被广泛使用，这些结果为该领域最常用的交叉频率耦合度量之一能够有效揭示神经协调的内容确立了一个重要边界。

## Abstract
Phase-amplitude coupling (PAC) is widely used to quantify interactions between neural oscillations across timescales and is often interpreted as reflecting temporally meaningful coordination between slow and fast neural activity. Combining empirical EEG data with mathematical analysis, we show that the modulation index (MI), a widely used PAC metric, remains unchanged when the phase of the low-frequency oscillation is inverted by 180{degrees}. By contrast, preferred phase rotates by exactly 180{degrees}, as expected. Thus, opposite temporal organisations of cross-frequency coupling can yield identical MI values. This finding defines a fundamental interpretational limit of MI-based PAC: MI quantifies the strength of phase-dependent amplitude modulation, but not its temporal polarity. Accordingly, MI alone cannot distinguish between temporally aligned and temporally inverted coupling configurations or support inferences about temporal alignment, phase polarity, or directionality. Because PAC is widely used across neuroscience, these results establish an important boundary on what one of the fields most common cross-frequency coupling measures can validly reveal about neural coordination.
