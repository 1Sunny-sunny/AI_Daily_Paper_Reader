## 摘要
双稳态神经群体模型通过稳定平衡点（代表正常神经活动）和稳定极限环（代表癫痫样振荡）的共存来描述与癫痫相关的转变。将系统驱动到平衡点吸引域之外的扰动可触发向癫痫样活动的转变。扩大该吸引域使系统能够从更大范围的扰动状态恢复至正常活动，为降低癫痫发作频率提供了一种基于模型的机制。受脑电刺激的启发，本文研究了利用脉冲反馈控制进行吸引域扩大的方法。结合高阶平均与周期性近恒等变换，我们建立了非零均值和零均值脉冲反馈增益扩大吸引域的条件，并推导出脉冲设计的指导原则。数值模拟验证了理论上的扩大。

## Abstract
Bistable neural population models describe transitions associated with epilepsy through the coexistence of a stable equilibrium representing normal neural activity and a stable limit cycle representing seizure-like oscillations. Perturbations that drive the system outside the equilibrium's basin of attraction can trigger transitions to seizure-like activity. Enlarging this basin enables the system to recover normal activity from a larger set of perturbed states, providing a model-based mechanism for reducing frequency of seizure initiation. Motivated by electrical brain stimulation, this paper investigates basin enlargement using pulsatile feedback control. Combining higher-order averaging with periodic near-identity transformations, we establish conditions under which nonzero-mean and zero-mean pulsatile feedback gains enlarge the basin of attraction and derive guidelines for pulse design. Numerical simulations validate the theoretical enlargement.
