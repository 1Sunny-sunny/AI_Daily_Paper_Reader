## 摘要
视觉到大脑编码预测视觉刺激诱发的神经响应，支持视觉认知研究和神经解码。然而，分离共享活动与图像特定活动的挑战，加上被试间分布偏移和表征异质性，限制了在有限校准数据下的准确编码和跨被试泛化。我们提出 SENSA，一个结构化连续神经响应生成框架，该框架将共享活动与图像依赖响应分离，并校准紧凑的被试状态。独立响应先验捕获共享活动，互补的视觉描述符驱动可分离的传感器-时间基和共享的响应精炼器。被试自适应因子迁移调整共享基的时空表达，而源锚定残差记忆在源响应坐标中预测图像依赖的校正。二者共同利用有限配对支持对冻结生成器进行个性化。实验表明，该生成骨干在三个数据集上优于近期生成器，图像身份扰动证实了刺激特异性。仅使用 400 张配对图像，脑电图（EEG）和自然场景数据集（NSD）校准分别达到 0.1091 和 0.2995 的残差皮尔逊相关系数，证明了有效的脑响应生成和跨被试自适应。

## Abstract
Visual-to-brain encoding predicts neural responses to visual stimuli, supporting visual cognition research and neural decoding. However, challenges in disentangling shared from image-specific activity, together with inter-subject distribution shifts and representational heterogeneity, limit accurate encoding and cross-subject generalization with limited calibration data.   We introduce SENSA, a structured continuous neural response generation framework that separates shared activity from image-dependent responses and calibrates a compact subject state. An independent response prior captures shared activity, while complementary visual descriptors drive separable sensor-time bases and a shared Response Refiner. Subject-Adaptive Factor Transport adjusts the spatiotemporal expression of shared bases, while Source-Grounded Residual Memory predicts image-dependent corrections in source-response coordinates. Together, they personalize a frozen generator using limited paired support.   Experiments demonstrate that the generation backbone outperforms recent generators across three datasets, with image-identity perturbations confirming stimulus specificity. Using only 400 paired images, electroencephalography (EEG) and the Natural Scenes Dataset (NSD) calibration achieve residual Pearson correlations of 0.1091 and 0.2995, respectively, demonstrating effective brain-response generation and cross-subject adaptation.
