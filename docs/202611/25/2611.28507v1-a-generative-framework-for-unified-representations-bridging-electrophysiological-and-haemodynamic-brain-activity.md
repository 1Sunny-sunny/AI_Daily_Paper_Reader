## 摘要
电生理与血流动力学记录在互补的时间与空间尺度上解析脑活动，然而配对采集仍难以大规模推广。我们提出一种生成式框架，在统一表征空间中对齐配对的多模态记录，并从脑电图生成模态特异的血流动力学响应。在配对队列中，该框架生成了能够复现区域动态、皮层图谱、功能连接和任务结构的fMRI BOLD与fNIRS血红蛋白响应。生成的BOLD信号遵循真实BOLD时间进程和皮层模式，而生成的血红蛋白响应追踪了任务诱发的HbO和HbR动态。该框架支持跨视觉、运动想象和心算任务的解码、独立记录解码、外部BOLD定位以及小样本增强。配对特征空间校准支持双向检索。当真实血流动力学数据稀缺时，所提框架将皮层分析和脑解码扩展到EEG记录。

## Abstract
Electrophysiological and haemodynamic recordings resolve brain activity at complementary temporal and spatial scales, yet paired acquisition remains difficult to scale. We propose a generative framework that aligns paired multimodal recordings in a unified representation space and generates modality-specific haemodynamic responses from EEG. In paired cohorts, the framework generated fMRI BOLD and fNIRS haemoglobin responses that reproduced regional dynamics, cortical maps, functional connectivity and task structure. Generated BOLD followed Real BOLD time courses and cortical patterns, while Generated haemoglobin responses tracked task-evoked HbO and HbR dynamics. The framework enabled decoding across visual, motor-imagery and mental-arithmetic tasks, independent-record decoding, external BOLD localization and low-sample augmentation. Paired feature-space calibration supported bidirectional retrieval. The proposed framework extends cortical analysis and brain decoding to EEG recordings when Real haemodynamic data are sparse.
