## 摘要
我们研究从单条含噪标量时间序列中重构未知动力系统。目标是恢复潜在动力学以进行预测。我们提出一种方法，利用微分嵌入坐标直接从数据中识别嵌入动力学的有理闭包。该闭包通过弱形式回归流程进行识别，避免了对含噪数据进行不稳定的逐点微分。当应用于无噪声的 Lorenz 和 Rössler 系统时，该方法恢复出的闭包能够在广泛的实现集合上支持长期预测（分别为 $18.1$ 和 $7.1$ 个李雅普诺夫时间）。在 $15\%$--$30\%$ 加性高斯噪声下，性能因系统而异。对于 Lorenz 系统，即使在最佳情况下，预测时域仍然较短；而 Rössler 系统在绝对意义上通常表现更好，但按李雅普诺夫时间归一化后则不再如此。我们提出的方法能够恢复出可直接解释的闭包系数，并将其与 Lorenz 和 Rössler 系统的已知解析闭包进行了比较。

## Abstract
We study the reconstruction of an unknown dynamical system from a single noisy scalar time series. The goal is to recover the underlying dynamics for forecasting. We introduce a method that uses differential embedding coordinates to identify a rational closure of the embedding dynamics directly from data. The closure is identified through a weak-form regression pipeline, which avoids unstable pointwise differentiation of noisy data. When applied to noise-free Lorenz and Rössler systems, the method recovers closures that support long forecasts across a broad ensemble of realizations ($18.1$ and $7.1$ Lyapunov times respectively). Under $15$--$30\%$ additive Gaussian noise, performance becomes system-dependent. For the Lorenz system, forecast horizons remain short even in the best cases, whereas the Rössler system generally performs better in absolute terms, though not once normalized by the Lyapunov time. Our proposed method recovers directly interpretable closure coefficients which we compared against the known analytic closures of the Lorenz and Rössler systems.
