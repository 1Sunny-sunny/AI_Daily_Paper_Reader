## 摘要
病理性神经元同步为研究网络化振荡系统中的稀疏去同步控制提供了一个实际动机，特别是在驱动与通信资源受限的应用中，例如深部脑刺激。受这一挑战驱动，我们研究如何在不采用连续更新控制的情况下镇定耦合振荡动力系统中的去同步，即使振荡器的相位不可用。我们建立了一个通用的事件触发控制框架，用于镇定耦合极限环振荡动力系统的去同步状态。我们的分析给出了一个统一的理论结果，表明去同步可以通过多种稀疏作用的反馈控制器实现，这些控制器仅依赖于序参量可观测量。所提出的控制器具有梯度下降解释，并能镇定一般相位约化振子网络的去同步状态。我们进一步证明，在事件触发机制下的受控系统具有严格正的事件间停留时间下界，从而排除了Zeno行为。为了解决实际中精确相位约化不可用的问题，我们引入了一种伪相位构造，该构造仅从状态测量即可得到可计算的序参量。对代表性振子网络的数值研究证明了所提框架的有效性和鲁棒性。

## Abstract
Pathological neuronal synchrony provides one practical motivation for studying sparse desynchronisation control in networked oscillatory systems, particularly in applications where actuation and communication are resource constrained, as in deep brain stimulation. Motivated by this challenge, we study how to stabilise desynchronisation in coupled oscillatory dynamical systems without continuously updated control, even when the oscillators' phase is unavailable. We develop a general event-triggered control framework for stabilising the desynchronised state of coupled limit-cycle oscillatory dynamical systems. Our analysis establishes a unified theoretical result showing that desynchronisation can be achieved by various feedback controllers that act sparsely and depend only on an order parameter observable. The proposed controllers admit a gradient-descent interpretation and stabilise the desynchronisation state of general phase-reduced oscillator networks. We further prove the controlled systems under event-triggered mechanisms possess a strictly positive lower bound of the inter-event dwell-time, excluding Zeno behaviour. To address the practical unavailability of exact phase reductions, we introduce a pseudo-phase construction that yields a computable order parameter from state measurements alone. Numerical studies on representative oscillator networks demonstrate the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed framework.
