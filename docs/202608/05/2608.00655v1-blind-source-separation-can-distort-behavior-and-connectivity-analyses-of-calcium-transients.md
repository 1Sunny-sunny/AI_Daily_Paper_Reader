## 摘要
去噪常被视为钙瞬变分析的技术前奏，但它可能重新定义行为解码与因果结构学习中所使用的变量。一项因可见伪影而启发的可检查运动神经元记录促使了本研究：移除与伪影关联的盲源分离（BSS）成分也会改变目标帧之外的轨迹动态。因此，我们检验了 BSS 去噪是否能在已提取的钙瞬变上保留行为与因果证据。在已知滞后图的合成基准测试中，原始受损轨迹能够恢复图结构，而去除成分的 BSS 变体则使跨估计器家族的中位图 F1 分数降至 0。接着，我们在四项幼虫斑马鱼 v2a 网状脊髓神经元（v2a-RSNs）与尾部行为记录上评估了四种 BSS 方法：FastICA、Infomax、SOBI 和 JADE。BSS 有时能改善行为解码，但增益取决于鱼个体、方法和保留的聚类；在交叉验证的倍率审计比较中，匹配的 PCA 和低通对照往往达到或超过 BSS。连接性效应更为一致：从 BSS 清理后的轨迹推断出的 c-GC 和 c-GC* 图比原始轨迹图密集得多。因此，BSS 应被视为对测量过程的干预，而非中性的清理步骤。

## Abstract
Denoising is often treated as a technical prelude to calcium transients analysis, but it can redefine the variables used for behavior decoding and causal structure learning. An inspectable motorneuron recording with a visible artefact motivated this study: removing artefact-linked blind source separation (BSS) components also changed trace dynamics outside the targeted frame. We therefore tested whether BSS denoising preserves behavior and causal evidence on already extracted calcium transients. In a synthetic benchmark with known lagged graphs, raw corrupted traces retained graph recovery, whereas component-removing BSS variants collapsed median graph F1 to 0 across estimator families. We then evaluated four BSS methods: FastICA, Infomax, SOBI, and JADE, on four larval zebrafish recordings of v2a reticulospinal neurons (v2a-RSNs) with tail behavior. BSS sometimes improved behavior decoding, but gains depended on fish, method and retained clusters; matched PCA and low-pass controls often matched or exceeded BSS in fold-audited comparisons. Connectivity effects were more consistent: c-GC and c-GC* graphs inferred from BSS-cleaned traces were much denser than raw-trace graphs. BSS should therefore be treated as an intervention on the measured process, not a neutral cleanup step.
