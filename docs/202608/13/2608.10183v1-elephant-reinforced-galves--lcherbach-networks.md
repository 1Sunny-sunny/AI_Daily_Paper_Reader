## 摘要
我们引入一个具有有界大象型强化突触相互作用的无限维 Galves–Löcherbach 系统。该强化机制修改兴奋性和抑制性突触更新的概率，同时保持其幅度一致有界。我们通过 Lyapunov 估计证明非爆炸性，并在耦合系统具有相同强化特征时建立膜电位动力学的条件 Wasserstein 收缩。最后，我们在 Poisson 假设下推导相应的复制平均场方程。

## Abstract
We introduce an infinite-dimensional Galves--Löcherbach system with bounded Elephant-type reinforced synaptic interactions. The reinforcement mechanism modifies the probabilities of excitatory and inhibitory synaptic updates while keeping their amplitudes uniformly bounded. We prove non-explosion by means of a Lyapunov estimate and establish a conditional Wasserstein contraction for the membrane-potential dynamics when the coupled systems share the same reinforcement profile. Finally, we derive the corresponding replica mean-field equation under the Poisson hypothesis.
