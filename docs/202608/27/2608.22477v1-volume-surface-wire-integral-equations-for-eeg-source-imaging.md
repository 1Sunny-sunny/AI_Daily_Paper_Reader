## 摘要
在脑电溯源成像中，脑活动成像的精度取决于前向头部模型的准确性，而后者又受到组织电导率表示的影响，包括颅骨和白质等各向异性区域。我们提出了一种基于混合体积-表面-线积分公式的脑电溯源成像框架，能够在无需对整个头部进行全体积离散的情况下，对白质的各向异性贡献进行纤维束感知建模。该框架在基于真实MRI的头部解剖结构上进行了评估，结果与有限元法（FEM）参考解高度一致，同时避免了全体积网格划分，并提供了白质纤维束的自然表示。随后，我们将该模型集成到一个实时流程中，该流程将脑电处理与沉浸式可视化相结合，能够同步检查头皮电位、皮层源以及白质纤维束相关量。结果支持所提出的公式作为经典公式的一种纤维束感知替代方案，兼具稳健的定位性能和交互式可视化能力。

## Abstract
In EEG source imaging, the precision of the imaging of the brain activity depends on the accuracy of the forward head model which is, in turn, affected by the representation of tissue conductivity, including anisotropic compartments such as the skull and white matter. We introduce an EEG source imaging framework based on a hybrid volume-surface-wire integral formulation, enabling tract-aware modeling of anisotropic white matter contributions without a full volumetric head discretization. The proposed framework is assessed on a realistic MRI-derived head anatomy, showing close agreement with FEM reference solutions while avoiding full-volume meshing and providing a natural representation of white matter fiber tracts. We then integrate this model into a real-time pipeline that couples EEG processing with immersive visualization, enabling synchronized inspection of scalp potentials, cortical sources, and white matter fiber tracts-related quantities. The results support the proposed formulation as a tract-aware alternative to canonical formulations, combining robust localization performance with interactive visualization capabilities.
