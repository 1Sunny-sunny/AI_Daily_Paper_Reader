## 摘要
电生理脑信号通常通过间接且含噪声的测量获取，提供潜在神经活动的变换表示。源重建——从这些测量中解析潜在神经信号的反问题——对于绘制脑功能图谱至关重要，但由于其不适定且对噪声敏感，仍然具有挑战性。深度学习方法在多种反问题中展现出潜力，然而许多方法并未明确纳入支配数据生成的生物物理原理，从而限制了数据效率和跨受试者适应性。在此，我们提出 DeepOp-Informed，一个融合生物物理信息的几何深度算子学习框架，通过自定义可微层将感知过程的生物物理机制嵌入模型中，从而实现更高效的学习并提升重建性能。该层使神经网络能够适应由脑解剖结构和传感器位置差异引起的信号生成物理过程在个体间的变化。在真实感脑磁图模拟中，DeepOp-Informed 能够泛化到留出受试者的前向模型，相较于多个神经网络基线和经典基线降低了重建误差。应用于青少年听觉诱发电位记录时，其产生了解剖上合理、定位于听觉皮层的重建结果。虽然我们的应用集中于脑磁图，但该框架具有通用性，或可适应其他成像模态。

## Abstract
Electrophysiological brain signals are typically acquired through indirect and noisy measurements, providing transformed representations of the underlying neural activity. Source reconstruction---the inverse problem of resolving underlying neural signals from these measurements---is essential for mapping brain function but remains challenging because it is ill-posed and sensitive to noise. Deep learning methods have shown promise across a range of inverse problems, yet many do not explicitly incorporate the biophysical principles governing data generation, limiting data efficiency and adaptation across subjects. Here, we introduce DeepOp-Informed, a biophysics-informed geometric deep operator learning framework that embeds the biophysics of the sensing process into the model through a custom differentiable layer, enabling more efficient learning and improved reconstruction performance. This layer enables the neural network to adapt to subject-specific variations in the physics of signal generation, resulting from differences in brain anatomy and sensor positioning. In realistic magnetoencephalography simulations, DeepOp-Informed generalizes to forward models from held-out subjects, reducing reconstruction error relative to several neural-network and classical baselines. Applied to adolescent auditory-evoked recordings, it produces anatomically plausible reconstructions localized to the auditory cortex. While our application focuses on magnetoencephalography, the framework is general and may be adaptable to other imaging modalities.
