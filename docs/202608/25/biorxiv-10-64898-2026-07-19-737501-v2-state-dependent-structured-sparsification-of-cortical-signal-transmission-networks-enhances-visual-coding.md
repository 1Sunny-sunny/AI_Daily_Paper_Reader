## 摘要
大脑能够根据行为情境逐刻快速调整感觉加工，而无需改变其底层的解剖连接。这种灵活性被认为源于信号传播所经由的有效网络的动态重构，然而控制这种重构的原理及其对编码的影响仍不清楚。在本研究中，我们利用同一记录时段内不同的静止和运动状态，通过在行为小鼠中以单神经元分辨率推断有向、毫秒尺度的信号传输网络来探讨这一问题。运动伴随着推断网络的反直觉的结构化稀疏化：相互作用变少但时间上更精确，而剩余的相互作用更加局部化、模块化、特征特异性和前馈性。然后，我们使用理论分析和多脑区发放率模型的受控扰动，系统地确定了这种重组中每个经验观察到的成分如何影响群体编码。我们发现，稀疏化减少了共享变异性；局部组织减少了信号-噪声对齐；特征特异性相互作用锐化了选择性；更前馈的架构加速了解码。这些结果提供了一种基于实验的机制，即在相同感觉输入下，行为状态通过该机制重组神经元相互作用，并提示结构化稀疏化是网络重构的一个基本原理，支持更准确和更快的感觉编码。

## Abstract
The brain can rapidly adjust sensory processing according to behavioral context from moment to moment without altering its underlying anatomical wiring. Such flexibility is thought to arise from dynamic reconfiguration of the effective network through which signals propagate, yet the principles governing such reconfiguration and its consequences for coding remain unclear. Here, we exploited the distinct stationary and locomotion states within the same recording sessions and addressed this question by inferring directed, millisecond-scale signal-transmission networks at single-neuron resolution in behaving mice. Locomotion was accompanied by a counterintuitive structured sparsification of the inferred network: interactions became fewer but more temporally precise, while the remaining interactions were more local, modular, feature-specific, and feedforward. We then used theoretical analysis and controlled perturbations of multi-area rate models to systematically determine how each empirically observed component of this reorganization affects population coding. We found that sparsification reduced shared variability; local organization reduced signal noise alignment; feature-specific interactions sharpened selectivity; and a more feedforward architecture accelerated decoding. These results provide an experimentally grounded mechanism by which behavioral state reorganizes neuronal interactions given the same sensory inputs, and suggest structured sparsification as an underlying principle of network reconfiguration that supports accurate and faster sensory coding.
