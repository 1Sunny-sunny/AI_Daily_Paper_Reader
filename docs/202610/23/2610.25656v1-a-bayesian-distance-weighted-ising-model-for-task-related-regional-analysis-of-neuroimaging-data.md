## 摘要
神经影像分析通常涉及对认知条件进行分类、识别任务相关脑区以及检查功能连接。这些目标通常被单独处理，缺乏基于模型的框架。相比之下，我们提出了一种贝叶斯距离加权伊辛模型，将分类、区域重要性和连接分析整合在一起。该模型基于功能信号之间的连接来刻画任务标签之间的依赖关系，而特定于感兴趣区（ROI）的交互参数则考虑了空间异质性，并量化了各区域对任务区分的贡献。然而，推断具有挑战性，因为信号可能在不相等的时间网格上观测，且似然函数包含难以处理的归一化常数。为了解决这一问题，我们采用了基展开和辅助变量马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗算法。我们将该方法应用于年轻和年长成年人的功能性近红外光谱数据，比较静息态和任务相关活动。该模型实现了较高的分类准确率，并揭示了区域重要性和连接的年龄依赖性模式。在年轻成年人中，左侧眶额皮层对任务区分的贡献强于背外侧前额叶和额极皮层，而年长成年人中区域差异不太明显。年长成年人还表现出对应感兴趣区之间更强的双侧连接，这与半球偏侧化减弱一致。

## Abstract
Neuroimaging analysis commonly involves classifying cognitive conditions, identifying task-relevant brain regions, and examining functional connectivity. These objectives are typically addressed separately, without a model-based framework. In contrast, we propose a Bayesian distance-weighted Ising model that integrates classification, regional importance, and connectivity analysis. The model captures dependence among task labels based on connectivities between functional signals, while region-of-interest (ROI)-specific interaction parameters account for spatial heterogeneity and quantify regional contributions to task discrimination. However, inference is challenging because the signals may be observed on unequal time grids and the likelihood function contains an intractable normalising constant. To address this, we use a basis expansion and an auxiliary variable Markov chain Monte Carlo algorithm. We apply the method to functional near-infrared spectroscopy data from younger and older adults, comparing resting-state and task-related activity. The model achieves high classification accuracy and reveals age-dependent patterns of regional importance and connectivity. Among younger adults, the left orbitofrontal cortex contributes more strongly to task discrimination than the dorsolateral prefrontal and frontopolar cortices, whereas regional differences are less evident among older adults. Older adults also exhibit greater bilateral connectivity between corresponding ROIs, consistent with reduced hemispheric lateralisation.
