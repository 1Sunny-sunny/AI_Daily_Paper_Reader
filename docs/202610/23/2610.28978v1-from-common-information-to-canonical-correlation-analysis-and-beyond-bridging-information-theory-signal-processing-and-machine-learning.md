## 摘要
信号处理、机器学习和神经影像中的许多问题会提供对同一过程的多个联合观测，每个观测都混合了一个所有观测共有的成分和一个其自身私有的成分，并且需要在无监督条件下恢复共有成分。这一问题已在多个研究群体中得到研究，且基本上各自独立。统计学家提出了典型相关分析（CCA），由 Hotelling 于 1936 年引入。信息论学者提出了两种共同信息概念，分别由 Gács 和 Körner 以及 Wyner 给出。工程师为传感器阵列和多用户通信开发了子空间方法，用于设计多视图接收机。本教程将各群体针对这一问题发展的主要结果和工具汇集于一份统一阐述中，并解释它们在交汇处所呈现的联系，区分精确等价、特例与类比。我们从第一性原理出发建立信息论基础。然后介绍 CCA 及其向多视图的扩展，接着梳理从盲多用户检测到作为盲接收机的 CCA 的工程脉络。接下来转向非线性 CCA，涵盖核方法、深度方法和概率形式化，讨论共同因子可识别的条件，以及与自监督学习的联系。最后讨论进一步的扩展和开放方向。本阐述是自足的，使来自上述任一群体的读者都能理解其他群体所发展的部分。

## Abstract
Many problems in signal processing, machine learning, and neuroimaging provide several jointly observed measurements of one process, each mixing a component common to all of them with a component private to itself, and require the common component to be recovered without supervision. This problem has been studied in several communities, largely independently. Statisticians proposed canonical correlation analysis (CCA), introduced by Hotelling in 1936. Information theorists proposed two notions of common information, due to Gács and Körner and to Wyner. Engineers developed subspace methods for sensor arrays and multiuser communications to design multiview receivers. This tutorial presents the main results and tools each community developed for this problem in a single exposition, and explains the connections that emerge where they meet, distinguishing exact equivalences from special cases and analogies. We develop the information-theoretic foundation from first principles. We then introduce CCA and its extension to several views, followed by the engineering thread from blind multiuser detection to CCA as a blind receiver. Next, we turn to nonlinear CCA, covering kernel, deep, and probabilistic formulations, the conditions under which the common factor is identifiable, and connections to self-supervised learning. We conclude with a discussion of further extensions and open directions. The exposition is self-contained, so that readers from each of these communities can follow the parts developed by the others.
