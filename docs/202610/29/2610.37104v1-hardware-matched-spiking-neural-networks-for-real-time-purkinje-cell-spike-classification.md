## 摘要
区分浦肯野细胞（PC）简单尖峰（SS）和复杂尖峰（CS）是研究小脑功能的关键。PC记录通常依赖于有线头部前置放大器，其系绳限制了自由活动动物的自然运动。无线头部前置放大器消除了这一限制，但在功耗预算内无法连续传输原始记录，因此在头部前置放大器上进行尖峰分类成为一种有吸引力的压缩方式，前提是它能在受限硬件上准确实时运行。本文提出了两种与其目标硬件相匹配的紧凑型脉冲神经网络（SNN）：ConvSNN映射到EFM32PG28微控制器的矩阵向量处理器（MVP），StreamSNN使用学习的时间常数和固定的突触延迟，为专用SKY130电路每个时间步处理一个波形值。在训练期间未见过的细胞上评估，ConvSNN的CS F1为0.873，参数为1,018个；StreamSNN的CS F1为0.837，参数为527个，两者均使用8位权重。在微控制器上，MVP加速将ConvSNN的延迟降低了62.3%，并在50 mAh电池上将估计电池寿命延长至33.73小时。在SKY130中，StreamSNN占用0.173 mm²，每个事件消耗0.46 μJ，其中0.2 ms的计算时间在8.3 ms的端到端延迟内。

## Abstract
Distinguishing Purkinje cell (PC) simple spikes (SSs) from complex spikes (CSs) is key to studying cerebellar function. PC recordings commonly rely on wired headstages, whose tethers restrict natural movement in freely behaving animals. Untethered headstages remove this constraint, but cannot continuously transmit raw recordings within their power budgets, making on-headstage spike classification an attractive form of compression, provided it runs accurately in real time on constrained hardware. This work presents two compact spiking neural networks (SNNs) matched to their target hardware: ConvSNN, which maps to the Matrix Vector Processor (MVP) of an EFM32PG28 microcontroller, and StreamSNN, which processes one waveform value per timestep using learned time constants and fixed synaptic delays for a dedicated SKY130 circuit. Evaluated on cells unseen during training, ConvSNN achieves a CS F1 of 0.873 with 1,018 parameters and StreamSNN 0.837 with 527 parameters, both with eight-bit weights. On the microcontroller, MVP acceleration reduces ConvSNN latency by 62.3\% and extends estimated battery life to 33.73~hours on a 50~mAh battery. In SKY130, StreamSNN occupies 0.173~mm$^2$ and consumes 0.46~$μ$J per event, with 0.2~ms of computation within an 8.3~ms end-to-end latency.
