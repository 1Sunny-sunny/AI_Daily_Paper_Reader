## 摘要
神经科学与机器学习的一个主要挑战是，在计算的因果解释中，将单神经元影响、群体动力学和回路连接联系起来。先前许多工作表明，低秩连接可以在训练过的人工神经网络中产生低维动力学，但这仍未阐明生物神经回路和许多人工神经元网络中高秩结构的功能相关性。在这里，我们分析了经过训练的循环神经网络，这些网络通过在一维和二维空间任务中整合连续变化的速度输入来定位奖励。我们发现主导的低维动力学编码任务位置，并且可以通过因果操纵来指导行为结果。然而，在分解底层回路后，我们发现虽然低秩连接可以解释低维动力学，但准确的性能和对新速度分布的泛化能力需要高秩连接。我们证明，这是通过分布式信号传导实现的，该信号传导纠正低维位置表示中的错误。因此，结合扰动、表征和回路层面的分析，展示了一种用于鲁棒空间计算的新机制，并表明神经回路中的高秩连接如何提供支持精确性与泛化能力的支架。

## Abstract
A major challenge in neuroscience and machine learning is to connect single-neuron influence, population dynamics, and circuit connectivity in a causal account of computation. Much previous work has shown that low-rank connectivity can generate low-dimensional dynamics in trained artificial neural networks, but this leaves unclear the functional relevance of the higher-rank structure of biological neural circuits and many artificial neuronal networks. Here we analyse recurrent neural networks trained to locate rewards by integrating continuously varying speed inputs in one- and two-dimensional spatial tasks. We find that dominant low-dimensional dynamics encode task locations, and can be causally manipulated to instruct behavioural outcomes. However, after decomposing the underlying circuitry we found that while low-rank connectivity accounts for the low-dimensional dynamics, accurate performance and generalisation to novel speed distributions requires high-rank connectivity. We demonstrate that this is achieved through distributed signalling that corrects errors in low-dimensional location representations. Thus, combined perturbation-, representation-, and circuit-level analyses demonstrate a novel mechanism for robust spatial computation and show how high-rank connectivity in neural circuits can provide a scaffold that supports precision and generalisation.
