## 摘要
皮层回路必须灵活响应新输入，同时整合过去的信息，但神经活动如何协调这些相互竞争的需求仍不清楚。结合来自六个小鼠视觉区的Neuropixels记录和机制性回路建模，我们识别出一种动力学状态，其中重尾超扩散涨落与长程时间依赖和振荡共存。我们使用有效平均场理论将这种状态形式化为自适应分数阶（AF）状态，其中空间和时间分数阶导数分别捕捉重尾偏移和长程记忆。我们发现表征AF状态的动力学指数在视觉层级中系统性变化：更高的视觉区表现出更弱的超扩散和更强的时间记忆。在回路模型中，这种层级变化源于有效抑制的逐渐减弱。这些发现将经典的时间尺度层级扩展为动力学状态层级，并表明AF状态使皮层区能够同时表达快速响应和长期时间整合。

## Abstract
Cortical circuits must respond flexibly to new inputs while integrating information about the past, yet the way in which neural activity reconciles these competing demands remains unclear. Combining Neuropixels recordings from six mouse visual areas with mechanistic circuit modeling, we identify a dynamical regime in which heavy-tailed superdiffusive fluctuations coexist with long-range temporal dependence and oscillations. We formalize this regime as the adaptive fractional (AF) state, using an effective mean-field theory in which spatial and temporal fractional derivatives capture heavy-tailed excursions and long-range memory, respectively. We find that dynamical exponents characterizing the AF state vary systematically across the visual hierarchy: higher visual areas exhibit weaker superdiffusion and stronger temporal memory. In the circuit model, this hierarchical shift emerges from a progressive weakening of effective inhibition. These findings extend the classical hierarchy of timescales to a hierarchy of dynamical regimes and suggest that the AF state allows cortical areas to jointly express rapid responses and long-term temporal integration.
