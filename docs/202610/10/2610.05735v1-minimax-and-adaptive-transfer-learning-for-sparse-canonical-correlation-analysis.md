## 摘要
我们针对具有多个异质性源数据集的高维稀疏典型相关分析（CCA）建立了一个迁移学习框架。其目标是通过借用相关来源的信息来改进目标典型系数矩阵的估计。我们通过联合协方差结构上的差异刻画源与目标之间的相似性，并在联合预测损失和分离预测损失下建立了极小极大最优速率，显式量化了有效样本量扩大带来的收益以及异质性带来的代价。为达到这些速率，我们首先基于去偏和截断归一化构造了一个oracle迁移估计量。随后，我们提出一种计算高效的方法，在未知稀疏水平的情况下自适应地达到极小极大速率：利用聚合来控制迁移偏差，并利用截断归一化方案在尺度敏感的预测损失下保持迁移收益。进一步提出了一种数据驱动的源检测方法来识别潜在有用的辅助数据集。我们的分析还给出了一个广义 sin θ 扰动定理，该定理去除了稀疏 CCA 问题中前导典型相关通常被施加的可比性条件。

## Abstract
We develop a transfer learning framework for high-dimensional sparse canonical correlation analysis (CCA) with multiple heterogeneous source datasets. Our goal is to improve estimation of the target canonical coefficient matrices by borrowing information from related sources. We characterize source-target similarity through discrepancies in the joint covariance structure and establish minimax optimal rates under both joint and separate prediction losses, explicitly quantifying the gain from enlarged effective sample size and the cost of heterogeneity. To attain these rates, we first construct an oracle transfer estimator based on debiasing and truncated normalization. We then develop a computationally efficient procedure that adaptively achieves the minimax rate without prior knowledge of the sparsity levels, using aggregation to control transfer bias and a truncated normalization scheme to preserve transfer gains under the scale-sensitive prediction loss. A data-driven source detection procedure is further proposed to identify potentially informative auxiliary datasets. Our analysis also yields a generalized sin-theta perturbation theorem that removes a commonly imposed comparability condition on leading canonical correlations in the sparse CCA problem.
