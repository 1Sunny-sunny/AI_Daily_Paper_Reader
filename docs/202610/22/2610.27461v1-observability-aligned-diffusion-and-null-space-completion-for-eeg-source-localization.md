## 摘要
电生理源成像严重欠定，因为导联场矩阵的秩相对于源维度有限且列之间高度相关。头皮脑电测量仅约束可观测的行空间分量，留下高维零空间不可观测。我们提出一个两阶段框架，将后验扩散细化与确定性零空间补全耦合。以最小范数初始化、奇异坐标的 Posterior-DDIM 采样器将时空扩散先验与方向相关的测量可靠性结合，以细化可观测分量。随后，冻结的岭回归预测器和非线性时间残差网络在显式投影约束下估计零分量，精确保持细化后的行估计及其预测脑电。所有学习组件仅在模拟皮层活动及其行-零分解上训练，无需配对脑电-源训练数据。在不同噪声水平的模拟中，与传统和基于学习的基线相比，该方法获得了更高的帧级受试者工作特征曲线下面积和平均精度，以及更低的定位误差。对诱发脑电和同步头皮脑电-立体定向脑电记录的探索性评估显示，与跨模态和颅内参考具有条件和事件依赖的一致性。

## Abstract
Electrophysiological source imaging is severely underdetermined because the lead-field matrix has limited rank relative to the source dimension and strongly correlated columns. Scalp EEG measurements constrain only the observable row-space component, leaving a high-dimensional null space unobservable. We propose a two-stage framework coupling posterior diffusion refinement with deterministic null-space completion. A minimum-norm-initialized, singular-coordinate Posterior-DDIM sampler combines a spatiotemporal diffusion prior with direction-dependent measurement reliability to refine the observable component. A frozen ridge predictor and nonlinear temporal residual network then estimate the null component under explicit projection constraints, preserving the refined row estimate and its predicted EEG exactly. All learned components are trained exclusively on simulated cortical activity and its row-null decomposition, without paired EEG-source training data. Across simulations with varying noise levels, the method achieved higher frame-level area under the receiver operating characteristic curve and average precision, together with lower localization error, than conventional and learning-based baselines. Exploratory evaluations on evoked EEG and simultaneous scalp EEG-stereo-EEG recordings showed condition- and event-dependent concordance with cross-modal and intracranial references.
