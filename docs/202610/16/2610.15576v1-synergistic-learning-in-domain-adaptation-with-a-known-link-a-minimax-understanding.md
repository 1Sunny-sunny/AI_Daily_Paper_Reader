## 摘要
何时将源数据与目标数据结合使用能比单独使用任一者带来显著更好的表现？从极小化极大观点出发，记 R(n,n_T) 为 n 个源观测和 n_T 个目标观测下的极小化极大风险，我们问何时 R(n,n_T) 的衰减严格快于 R(n,0) 和 R(0,n_T)。我们将此称为协同学习现象（SLP），并用协同优势比 SAR=min(R(n,0),R(0,n_T))/R(n,n_T) 加以量化。我们研究具有已知关联的领域自适应：源回归 f，目标回归 g=Af（A 为已知算子），设计可能不同。三种关联——恒等、积分和微分——以及密度比 ρ=h_T/h_S 决定协同是否可能、以何种速率以及在何种样本量窗口内发生。对于恒等关联（协变量偏移），在源为 Beta(a,1)、目标为均匀分布的情形下，SLP 的一个已知必要条件为 a≥2+1/(2β)，其中 β 是 f 的光滑度（结果 I）。积分关联 g(x)=∫_0^x f(t)dt 即使密度比有界也可产生联合速率 (n n_T)^{-1/2}（结果 II），并且在源为 Beta(a,1) 时具有以 a=2 和 a=3 为分界的相图：1<a<2 时为积分型 SLP，2<a<3 时额外出现水平分裂速率 n_T^{-1}，a>3 时为协变量偏移型空间 SLP（结果 III）。导数关联 g=\dot f 仅在更强的奇异性下产生协同（结果 IV），从而在有界密度比下呈现清晰的积分—微分不对称性。在所有三种关联中，协同源于角色分离：每个数据集解决与关联和设计相关的不同瓶颈。我们刻画了协同的样本量窗口以及 SAR 如何随 n 和 n_T 变化。模拟支持所预测的相结构，并且对真实车辆和智能手机信号的镜像积分—微分分析展示了这种不对称性。

## Abstract
When can combining source and target data yield much better performance than using either alone? From a minimax viewpoint, writing $R(n,n_T)$ for the minimax risk from $n$ source and $n_T$ target observations, we ask when $R(n,n_T)$ decays strictly faster than both $R(n,0)$ and $R(0,n_T)$. We call this the Synergistic Learning Phenomenon (SLP) and quantify it by the Synergistic Advantage Ratio $\mathrm{SAR}=\min(R(n,0),R(0,n_T))/R(n,n_T)$. We study domain adaptation with a known link: source regression $f$, target regression $g=\mathcal{A}f$ for a known operator $\mathcal{A}$, and possibly different designs. Three links---identity, integration, and differentiation---and the density ratio $ρ=h_T/h_S$ determine whether synergy is possible, at what rate, and in which sample-size window. For the identity link (covariate shift) with a Beta$(a,1)$ source and uniform target, a known necessary condition for SLP is $a\geq2+1/(2β)$, where $β$ is the smoothness of $f$ (Result I). An integral link $g(x)=\int_0^xf(t)\,dt$ can yield the joint rate $(nn_T)^{-1/2}$ even for a bounded ratio (Result II), and under a Beta$(a,1)$ source has a phase diagram with cuts at $a=2$ and $a=3$: integral SLP for $1<a<2$, an extra level-split rate $n_T^{-1}$ for $2<a<3$, and covariate-shift-type spatial SLP for $a>3$ (Result III). A derivative link $g=\dot f$ synergizes only under a stronger singularity (Result IV), giving a sharp integration--differentiation asymmetry under a bounded ratio. Across all three links, synergy arises from role separation: each dataset resolves a distinct bottleneck associated with the link and designs. We characterize the sample-size windows for synergy and how SAR varies with $n$ and $n_T$. Simulations support the predicted phase structure, and a mirrored integration--differentiation analysis of real vehicle and smartphone signals illustrates the asymmetry.
