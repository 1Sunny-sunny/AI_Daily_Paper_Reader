## 摘要
利用脉冲模型挖掘具有高时间依赖性的数据集（如语音关键词识别）中的时间信息仍然是一个未决问题。有前景的框架包括首次脉冲时间编码（TTFS）以及在学习中引入突触延迟。在 TTFS 中，神经元最多只能发放一次脉冲，迫使网络基于神经元的发放时间而非发放率进行学习。一些模型使用 TTFS 框架直接计算整合-发放（IF）或泄漏整合-发放（LIF）神经元的脉冲发放时间。然而，大多数基于 TTFS 框架构建的模型尚未在包含时间信息的数据集上进行测试。关于这些模型在时间数据集上的延迟，人们知之甚少；这里的延迟是指网络正确分类一个样本所需的时间。在本工作中，我们研究延迟神经网络（DeNN）模型的行为，即那些绕过脉冲神经元的膜电位和阈值机制、直接计算神经元脉冲发放时间的模型。我们展示了如何解析地控制其延迟，并在动态时间数据集上实证研究了延迟与准确率之间的权衡。

## Abstract
Exploiting temporal information in datasets with high temporal dependencies (such as spoken keyword recognition) by spiking models is still an open problem. Promising frameworks include Time-To-First coding (TTFS) and the inclusion of synaptic delays into learning. In TTFS, neurons are forced to spike at most once, forcing the network to found its learning on the spiking times of neurons rather than their spiking rate. Some models use the TTFS framework to directly compute the spiking time of Integrate-and-Fire (IF) or Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) neurons. However, most models built in the TTFS framework have not been tested against datasets containing temporal information. Little is known about the latency of these models on temporal datasets, where the latency is the time taken by the network to correctly classify a sample. In this work, we study the behaviour of Delay Neural Networks (DeNN) models, i.e. models that directly compute the spiking time of neurons by bypassing the membrane potential and thresholding mechanisms of spiking neurons. We show how to analytically control their latency, and empirically study the latency-accuracy tradeoff in a dynamic temporal dataset.
