## 摘要
本文重访用于序列数据分析的熵及相关量（熵密度、有效测度复杂度和信息距离）。我们在信息论与遍历论的框架下给出其数学基础，建立概率性香农熵与算法性柯尔莫哥洛夫复杂度之间的联系。核心结果 Ziv 定理证明了归一化不同词数与熵密度之间的渐近等价性，为通过 Lempel-Ziv 分解进行估计提供了理论基础。我们考察这些量的收敛性质，并介绍有限序列的估计方法，包括有效测度复杂度和转移熵率的估计方法。我们介绍了一个开源软件库，该库实现了用于高效 Lempel-Ziv 分解的并行算法。通过三个案例研究展示该框架的实际用途：基于 fMRI 数据的功能性脑连接分析、耦合非线性振子的时空动力学刻画，以及基于心电图的房颤分类，说明熵测度如何在不同领域中捕获有意义的结构与动力学性质。

## Abstract
This article revisits entropy and related magnitudes (entropy density, effective measure complexity, and informational distance) for the analysis of sequential data. We present their mathematical foundations within information theory and ergodic theory, establishing connections between probabilistic Shannon entropy and algorithmic Kolmogorov complexity. A central result, Zivs theorem, demonstrates the asymptotic equivalence between the normalised number of distinct words and entropy density, providing the theoretical basis for estimation via Lempel-Ziv factorisation. We examine the convergence properties of these magnitudes and introduce estimation procedures for finite sequences, including a method for the effective measure complexity and the transfer entropy rate. An open-source software library implementing parallel algorithms for efficient Lempel-Ziv factorisation is presented. The practical utility of the framework is illustrated through three case studies: functional brain connectivity analysis from fMRI data, characterisation of spatiotemporal dynamics in coupled nonlinear oscillators, and electrocardiogram-based classification of atrial fibrillation, demonstrating how entropic measures capture meaningful structural and dynamical properties across diverse domains.
