## 摘要
连接组约束的神经网络使生物组件可直接用于干预，但其机制性结论仍可能依赖于比较模型、干预方式、回路表示和训练得到的解。我们在果蝇运动视觉模型 flyvis 中，利用其 50 个模型的集成、任务匹配的因果 CNN 对照以及重复干预，对这些依赖性进行压力测试。在模型行为层面，flyvis 中的三级 reverse-phi 反转强烈依赖于周期和速度，并且也出现在任务匹配的 CNN 中；两个模型家族都未能复现果蝇的静止锯齿波响应。对抗性方向反转在 flyvis 中需要扰动边界为刺激对比度的 0.21--0.27 倍，而在 CNN 中为 0.13--0.25 倍；但与参数匹配的 CNN 相比，这一差距在更大的 CNN 中基本消失。在机制层面，将归因与干预方式匹配很重要：对于留出均值替换，一阶边归因修补达到汇总 AUROC 0.970 和 Spearman 0.872；而其零对齐分数在相同真值上评估时仅为 0.933 和 0.126。回路结论也因表示和集成成员而异。贪婪细胞类型集合在 50 个模型中的 25 个中满足模型相对的 T4/T5 调谐标准，而在 40 个模型中，T4/T5 输出沉默将受测光流行为降低到八个片段中至多两个。在细胞类型搜索首先成功的六个模型中，相应连接集合可被剪枝至 13--31 个新增类别；在额外的双侧响应幅度检查下，这六个首次成功的细胞类型集合中只有两个仍满足调谐阈值。这些结果支持对连接组约束模型采取更窄的使用方式：它们使机制性假设可直接检验，但由此得出的主张必须限定在模型家族、干预方式、表示和训练得到的解的范围之内。

## Abstract
Connectome-constrained neural networks make biological components directly accessible to intervention, but their mechanistic conclusions can still depend on the comparison model, intervention, circuit representation and trained solution. We stress-test these dependencies in flyvis, a \emph{Drosophila} motion-vision model, using its 50-model ensemble, task-matched causal CNN controls and replicated interventions. At the level of model behaviour, three-level reverse-phi reversal in flyvis is strongly period- and velocity-dependent and also appears in task-matched CNNs; neither family reproduces the fly's stationary-sawtooth response. Adversarial direction reversal requires a perturbation bound of 0.21--0.27 times stimulus contrast in flyvis versus 0.13--0.25 in CNNs, but the gap over a parameter-matched CNN largely closes for larger CNNs. At the level of mechanism, matching attribution to the intervention matters: for held-out-mean replacement, first-order edge attribution patching reaches pooled AUROC 0.970 and Spearman 0.872, versus 0.933 and 0.126 when its zero-aligned score is evaluated against the same ground truth. Circuit conclusions also vary with representation and ensemble member. Greedy cell-type sets meet a model-relative T4/T5 tuning criterion in 25 of 50 models, while T4/T5 output silencing reduces the tested optic-flow behaviour to at most two of eight clips in 40. In six models where the cell-type search first succeeds, the corresponding connection sets can be pruned to 13--31 added classes; under an additional two-sided response-amplitude check, only two of those six first-success cell-type sets still meet the tuning threshold. These results support a narrower use of connectome-constrained models: they make mechanistic hypotheses directly testable, but the resulting claims must be scoped to the model family, intervention, representation and trained solution.
